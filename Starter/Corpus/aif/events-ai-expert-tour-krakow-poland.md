# AI Expert Tour – Kraków

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AI-Expert-Tour_960x520.jpg)

## Event details

Location: Cluster for Social and Economic Innovation Zabłocie 20.22, Kraków, Poland

Date: 3.9.2026

Time: 9:00–12:30 CEST

The next stop on our AI Expert Tour is Kraków on 3 September!

How can companies start their AI journey? Where can they find the right skills, funding opportunities, and expert support? And how can they avoid the most common pitfalls along the way?

Join us for a business breakfast that will bring together company representatives, business support organizations, and AI experts to discuss practical ways of leveraging artificial intelligence for business growth and innovation.

## Why attend?

- Learn about the current opportunities available to support companies in adopting and developing AI solutions.
- Discover the specific support services offered by the LUMI AI Factory project, including free expert consultations, training programs, and access to advanced computing infrastructure.
- Meet experts specializing in AI implementation and business transformation.
- Hear practical case studies from companies benefiting from the innovation support ecosystem.
- Connect with professionals from the business and technology sectors.
- Participate in networking sessions and open discussions with experts.
