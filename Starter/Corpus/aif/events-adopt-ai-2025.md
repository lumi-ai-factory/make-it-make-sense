# Adopt AI

![Adopt AI Paris Event November 25-26](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1743605464358-960x540.jpeg)

## Event details

Place:  Grand Palais, Paris, France

Date: 25.-26.11.2025

Meet us at Adopt AI!

Be part of one of the largest AI event of the year on November 25-26, 2025, at the prestigious Grand Palais – RMN (Officiel) in Paris. LUMI AI Factory will join the Embassy of Finland in Paris at their booth during the summit, alongside leading Finnish innovators: **AI Finland, Aiforia, Confidentialmind, Indalgo, Relex, and Sprint AI**.

The opening hours of the summit for the public:

- Tuesday 25 November 8.30 – 21.00 (presence at stands until 18.30)
- Wednesday 26 November 8.30 – 21.00 (presence at stands until 18.30)

Adopt AI exists to accelerate the adoption of artificial intelligence across industries, driving tangible business transformation. The Adopt AI will bring together 25,000 attendees and 500+ world-class speakers, including Fortune 500 CEOs, AI startup founders, industry leaders, researchers, and policymakers.
