# Finland’s Digital Compass: What’s Next for AI?

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LUMI-AIFlogo_1200x630-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Place: LUMI AI Factory Hub Otaniemi, 4th floor, Maarintie 8, Espoo, Finland

Date: 15.9.2026

Time: 15:00–16:00 EEST

How will Finland’s updated Digital Compass shape the future of AI?

Join us at the [LUMI AI Factory Hub Otaniemi](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/finland-lumi-ai-factory-hubs/#otaniemi) to hear **Tomi Halonen** (Ministry of Education and Culture) discuss Finland’s AI strategy, priorities, and vision for a human-centric AI society.

The event includes a presentation discussion, and networking opportunities for students, researchers, companies, startups, public sector professionals and other stakeholders interested in learning more about and contributing to Finland’s AI vision.

Come and join the conversation on how Finland plans to shape the next chapter of AI!
