# Nordic Supercomputing Summit 2026

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/NSS_950x500_LAIF.jpg)

## Event details

Location: Sokos Hotel Tripla, Fredikanterassi 1 B, Helsinki, Finland

Date: 17.11.2026

Time: 9:00–17:45 EET

Meet Nordic experts in HPC, quantum computing and AI at the Nordic Supercomputing Summit 2026!

This event is designed for curious minds – R&D-driven innovators, technology leaders and business decision-makers who want to stay ahead of industry trends, expand their networks and explore new growth and innovation opportunities.

Be among the first to hear about the latest cutting-edge technology opportunities for companies through keynotes, company cases and hands-on sessions — followed by networking.

This event is for you if you work in demanding simulations, AI development, digital twins or quantum computing or are interested in their benefits for your company.
