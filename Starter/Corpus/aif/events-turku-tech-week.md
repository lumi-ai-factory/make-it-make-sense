# Turku Tech Week

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Turku_Tech_Week_2026_EN_1920x1080-1536x864-1-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Place: Turku, Finland

Date: 2.–6.3.2026

Come and meet us at Turku Tech Week!

[Business Turku](https://businessturku.fi/en/) is accelerating innovation and business development by organising Turku Tech Week on 2–6 March 2026. Held in the Turku Science Park area, the week’s program is built around timely and relevant themes.

Check out the “**How to Extract Value from Data**” session by Morthen Mathisen, CSC, at the Opening Event on Monday 2 March.

**Time:** 8:30–11:00 EET (“How to Extract Value from Data” starting at 9:50 EET)
**Venue:** Visitor and Innovation Center Joki (Lemminkäisenkatu 12b, Turku) 
**Lecture Hall:** Cave

On Tuesday 3 March, LUMI AI Factory will be holding a showcase at the “Advanced Technologies as Accelerators of Business Growth” event. There will be three tables with different topics:

- Table 1 “Getting started with LUMI AI Factory / LUMI Hardware”
- Table 2 “LUMI services – Web services and scheduling services”
- Table 3 “Using LUMI for AI – Software and tasks”

**Time:** 8:30–12:00 EET 
**Venue:** Visitor and Innovation Center Joki
**Lecture Hall:** Putous (2nd floor)

## Read more about the event

All Turku Tech Week events are open and free of charge, but most require advance registration.
