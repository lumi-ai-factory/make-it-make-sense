## Event details

Location: Dipoli, Otakaari 24, Espoo, Finland

Date: 16.–17.9.2026

Join the LUMI AI Factory at NORDEEP and connect with our experts working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and high-performance computing!

NORDEEP Nordic Deep Tech Business Summit is a meeting spot for startups, investors, corporations, universities and research institutions, accelerators, public organisations, and other key stakeholders from across the entire deep tech value chain.

At the event, you can also learn about the computing resources, expertise and other services available through the LUMI AI Factory, and discover how we can support each stage of your AI development.

Come and visit our booth at the largest deep tech business summit in the Nordics!
