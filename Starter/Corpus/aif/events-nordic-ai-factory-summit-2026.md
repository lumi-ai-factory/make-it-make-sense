# Nordic AI Factory Summit 2026

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/banner_-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Location: Hotel Birger Jarl, Stockholm, Sweden

Date: 13.10.2026

Time: 8:00–17:30 CEST

Join us at Nordic AI Factory Summit 2026!

Nordic AI Factory Summit is a meeting point for the organisations, researchers, and policymakers shaping how AI is built, scaled, and governed across Europe. Anchored in the momentum of the EuroHPC AI Factory initiative, the summit connects AI infrastructure with real‑world deployment across industry, academia, and the public sector.

Come and meet the LUMI AI Factory team at the summit, where we will take part in the program.

**Workshop – AI agents with the LUMI supercomputer**
Unlock new levels of efficiency on LUMI with Agentic AI. This workshop covers the foundational concepts and practical architectures needed to safely deploy AI agents to assist with code development, job management, and system documentation in a high performance computing (HPC) environment.

You can also connect with us at our booth – discover how our services can help you accelerate your AI innovation at the Nordic AI Factory Summit 2026!
