# 6G Resilience Summit

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/6G-960x540.png)

## Event details

Location: Crowne Plaza Helsinki Hesperia, Helsinki, Finland

Date: 27.10.2026

Join us at 6G Resilience Summit!

6G Resilience Summit returns in 2026 to unite global leaders, innovators, and policymakers in shaping the next generation of resilient, secure, and sustainable 6G networks. As 6G moves from vision to reality, this summit will explore critical challenges ranging from AI-driven network resilience to standardisation.

In the morning session starting at 9:00 EET, our Business Domain Manager **Jasmine Holmström** will be giving a presentation on how LUMI AI Factory can support resilience in AI-native 6G.

You can also come and meet us at our booth. Discover how we can support each stage of your AI development with the services we offer – free of charge for SMEs and startups!
