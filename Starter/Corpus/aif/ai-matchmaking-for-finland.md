# AI Matchmaking for Finland

*LUMI AI Factory offers access to the 10x Matchmaking platform operated by AI Finland. 10x Matchmaking helps organisations in Finland turn AI-related challenges and development needs into opportunities for collaboration. Users describe a need or present an offering, and an AI-assisted workflow identifies relevant connections across the ecosystem. Matches can connect needs with products and services, experts, research, funding opportunities, references and organisations addressing similar challenges.*

## Service features

1. Create an account for your organisation and describe your AI needs with the support of the platform’s AI assistant.

2. Receive tailored partner recommendations, including compatibility scores and explanations of why the match may be relevant to your needs.

3. Connect directly with potential partners through the platform and explore collaboration opportunities.

Eligibility: The service is available only to organisations established in Finland.

## Target groups

- Organisations based in Finland seeking to adopt, develop, or scale the use of AI. This includes companies, municipalities, wellbeing services counties, educational institutions, and other public sector organisations looking for AI solutions, expertise, and collaboration opportunities.

- AI ecosystem actors contributing to the development of Finland’s AI landscape, including AI solution providers, consultants, universities, research organisations, and other innovation partners seeking to support organisations in their AI journey.

## Pricing

The service if free of charge to eligible organisations.

## Request process

Go to: <https://10x.aifinland.fi>

- Register or sign in with your personal account.

- Create or join your organisation and complete its profile.

- Describe your AI need, desired outcome and requirements, or add your solution or expertise.

- Review suggested matches and their explanations.

- Request an introduction and continue the discussion with a potential partner.
