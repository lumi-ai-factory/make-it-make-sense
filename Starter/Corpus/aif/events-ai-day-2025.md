# AI Day 2025

![AI Day 2025 Event Image](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/AI_DAY_2025.png-960x540.webp)

## Event details

Place:  Dipoli, Aalto University, Espoo, Finland

Date: 13.11.2025

Time: 9:00 – 18:00 EET

Meet us at AI Day 2025!

AI Day is the biggest annual event highlighting the leading AI research in Finland. The event brings together researchers, companies, students and the public sector involved in the fast-developing field of AI. AI Day promotes matchmaking, information sharing, and cross-border collaboration —making it the perfect place to connect and learn.

Come and meet us and discover how we can support your AI journey. With hands-on expert guidance, tailored training, and access to advanced computing resources, LUMI AI Factory helps you strengthen AI skills, develop innovative solutions, and achieve ambitious goals.

Don’t miss **Aleksi Kallio’s,** the director of LUMI AI Factory services, presentation: “**LUMI AI Factory: Turning AI Supercomputing into Economic Impact**” in the second industry session at 2-3pm. Join the session to explore how Europe’s world-class computing power, data, and talent can become a launchpad for entirely new AI-driven businesses. LUMI AI Factory does just that — accelerating AI adoption across domains such as manufacturing, health, and communication technologies, while empowering start-ups and SMEs to innovate faster and compete globally. By combining sustainable operations, trustworthy AI, and close collaboration between industry, research, and public partners, LUMI AI Factory transforms breakthrough ideas into real economic impact and societal benefit.
