# Kainuu Digital Summit 2025

![Kainuu Digital Summit 2025 Sponsored by: Loiste Al Empowering Business, Academia, and Startups 5th- 6th November, Kajaani Register now!](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/16x9_-KDS_25-program-_Register_Presentation-2-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Place:  Conference Center Kaukametsä (Kouta-sali), Kajaani, Finland

Date: 6.11.2025

Time: 8:30-16:00 EET

Join us at Kainuu Digital Summit 2025 in Kajaani – the home of the LUMI supercomputer.

Join the summit and find out how AI is being implemented across industry sectors with concrete examples that demonstrate its transformative potential. Whether you’re a technology enthusiast or a business leader seeking to understand the strategic value of AI, this event offers a platform for learning, collaboration, and inspiration!

The LUMI AI Factory will be present at the summit. Come and learn more about the AI Factory and the services we offer through our presentation or by visiting our booth. There’s also a unique chance to visit the LUMI data center.
