# Survey: Needs, experiences, and priorities of organisations working with AI and HPC

1 min read

11.6.2026

 ![We want to hear from you!](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/SoMe_1200X630_v2-960x540.jpg)

We are conducting a survey to better understand the needs, experiences, and priorities of organisations working with artificial intelligence (AI) and high‑performance computing (HPC).

LUMI AI Factory users and collaborators – we want to hear from you about your experiences and needs related to our compute, training, and consulting services, whether you have actively used them or only had initial contact with our team. Your feedback will help us improve our services, infrastructure, and support, and will shed light on emerging trends, use cases, and barriers across different sectors.

All data will be handled in anonymised and aggregated form. The information collected will be used solely to improve services and understand trends within the LUMI AI Factory. Answering takes approximately 15 minutes, and the deadline is June 30th. [Fill the survey here!](https://link.webropolsurveys.com/Participation/Public/970e144d-0ddb-492f-bd6b-d35d530d2267?displayId=Fin3677337)
