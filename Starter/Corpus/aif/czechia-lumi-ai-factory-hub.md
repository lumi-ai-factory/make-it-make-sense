# Czechia LUMI AI Factory Hub

### **LUMI AI Factory Hub Ostrava** is a modern, flexible workspace located near IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center designed to provide coworking space and connect AI startups, SMEs researchers, innovators, and students to address artificial intelligence challenges.

The Hub offers versatile facilities for the AI community.

- **Resident Facilities:** Options for long-term team residency as well as short-term access via Day Passes.
- **Shared Coworking Space:** A total of 32 workstations, comprising fixed desks with monitors for privacy and community tables for collaboration.
- **Event Spaces:** Adaptable areas within IT4Innovations suitable for large-scale workshops, expert training sessions, and presentations.
- **The AI Campus**: a dedicated physical and virtual ecosystem designed to support students at all levels of higher education (BSc, MSc, and PhD) enrolled in AI-related programs. The AI Campus provides an engaging environment for developing local AI talent and strengthening the connection between academic education and real-world AI and supercomputing research.

[Read more about the LUMI AI Factory Hub policies](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/hubs/)

#### WHAT WE OFFER

- **Expert Support & Ecosystem:** Provides direct access to LUMI AI Factory services and integration into the growing Czech and European AI landscape.
- **Professional Networking:** Focuses on connecting startups and SMEs with scientific experts through regular community events and workshops.
- **Experimental Zone:** A dedicated space for implementing and scaling projects that use AI as a core technology or to solve specific technical pain points.

## How to become a member

## eligibility

To determine if an applicant is a suitable candidate, we evaluate their alignment with our mission of fostering responsible AI innovation and their potential to actively contribute to the hub’s ecosystem.

## Selection process

Selection is based on a structured internal system that assesses technical depth, professional reliability, and the growth potential of the proposed AI project

We prioritise teams whose work represents genuine AI applications – whether as a core service or a solution to a critical pain point – ensuring they can both benefit from and add value to our community.

## evaluation

The evaluation is weighted across several key categories, including relevance to the LUMI AI Factory mission (10%), technical depth (20%), and the potential for community engagement (20%)

Candidates must also meet essential eligibility criteria, such as being based in a EuroHPC JU participating country and holding a valid company registration number.
