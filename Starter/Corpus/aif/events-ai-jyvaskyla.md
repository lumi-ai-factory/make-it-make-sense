# AI Jyväskylä

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AI_Finland-960x523.webp)

## Event details

Location: Piippukatu 2 (Dynamo) 40100 Jyväskylä, Finland

Date: 09.09.2026

Time: 08:30 – 15:00

How can the manufacturing industry use artificial intelligence to build a sustainable competitive advantage safely, efficiently, and effectively?

AI Jyväskylä – The “Functional and Secure Artificial Intelligence” event brings together companies in the manufacturing industry, as well as AI experts and researchers, to discuss and share experiences on how AI can be practically utilised to develop business operations and competitiveness. We look forward to seeing you there!

Visit our booth 11:00-14:00 to hear more about the possibilities LUMI AI Factory offers!

Please note that the language of the event is Finnish.

## Read more about the event and register

During the AI Jyväskylä – Functional and Secure Artificial Intelligence event, you’ll hear practical examples and expert presentations on the use and management of artificial intelligence. The event also offers you an excellent opportunity to network. Admission is free.
