# CodeRefinery workshop

## Event details

Location: Aalto University,
Espoo, Finland

Date: 17.3.–19.3.2026

Time: 11:00–16:00 EET

As part of the March 2026 CodeRefinery workshop, there will be a local in-person session for days 1–3 (March 17-19) at the Otaniemi AI Factory Hub.

The CodeRefinery workshop is an interactive 6 half-day online event with some local in-person sessions. The goal of the event is to get familiar with research software development tools and practices. If you are writing code that is used in research, then this workshop is for you!

One of the in-person sessions will be held at the Otaniemi AI Factory Hub (Maarintie 8, Espoo, Finland). The session is open to anyone in Finland who has registered to the workshop. You will get a separate registration form for the in-person session, but please remember to also register to the workshop.

## Read more about the training

Find the full program and further information on CodeRefinery’s website.

The workshop is free of charge, but please remember to register.
