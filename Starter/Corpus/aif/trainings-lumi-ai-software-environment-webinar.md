# LUMI AI Software Environment webinar

## Event details

Location: Online

Date: 25.3.2026

Time: 13:00 CET

Join us on Wednesday 25 March 2026 at 13:00 CET (14:00 EET)for a presentation on Artificial Intelligence Software Environmentby Marlon Tobaben and Mitja Sainio (CSC / LUMI AI Factory) via Zoom.

The [AI Software Environment](https://docs.lumi-supercomputer.eu/laif/software/ai-environment/) by the LUMI AI Factory is a comprehensive, ready-to-use containerised stack for AI and machine learning workloads on the LUMI supercomputer. These containers are the future for running machine learning frameworks like PyTorch on LUMI.
