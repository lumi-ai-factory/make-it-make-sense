# LUMI AIF DaaS nominated for Data Economy Spotlight 2026 listing

2 mins read

28.5.2026

 ![Data Economy Spotlight 2026](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/data_economy_spotlight_2026-960x540.jpg)

The LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service (DaaS) was nominated for [the Data Economy Spotlight 2026 listing](https://www.dataspacesalliance.fi/en/data-economy-spotlight-2026-list/) (Datatalouden kiinnostavimmat) by Data Spaces Alliance Finland!

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/60E0FFFB-F63C-49FF-A4E2-3B714A119EAA-800x800.jpg)

The Data Spaces Alliance Finland network accelerates data economy development by compiling a list of Finland’s most compelling data solutions.

In the expert jury’s assessment, specific emphasis was placed on solutions that remove barriers to innovation and lay the groundwork for new, scalable business models. The list also features a few solutions that were already recognised in 2023; the Finnish data economy has matured, and these top-tier solutions have evolved technically, expanded into new markets, and proven their ability to generate sustained value.

Read the whole listing [here](https://www.dataspacesalliance.fi/en/data-economy-spotlight-2026-list/).
