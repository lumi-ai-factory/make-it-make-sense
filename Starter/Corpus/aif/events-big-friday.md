# Big Friday

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/BIG-FRIDAY-2026.webp)

## Event details

Place: Kajaani, Finland

Date: 30.1.2026

Time: 15:30-23:00 EET

Join us at Big Friday!

Kajaani´s famous Big Friday will be back on Friday 30.1.2026 at Kajaanihalli.

This year, Big Friday will dive into how AI is changing business, creativity and working life — and above all, how each of us can utilize this transformation in our own work.

Together with AIKA, LUMI AI Factory will take over one of the event oases to showcase the services and opportunities we offer. Come meet us in Kajaanihalli and explore how AI is shaping tomorrow!
