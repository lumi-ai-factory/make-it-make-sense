# ISC 2026

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ISC_2026-960x540.png)

## Event details

Place: Congress Center Hamburg, Germany

Date: 23.6.–25.6.2026

Join us at ISC 2026 and discover the power of the LUMI AI Factory and the LUMI supercomputer.

One of nineteen AI Factories established by the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking, the LUMI AI Factory combines cutting-edge computing power, rich datasets, and top-tier AI expertise to deliver a pioneering open AI platform. It is set to become a premier hub for AI excellence, empowering businesses, researchers, and innovators in an unprecedented way.

The LUMI supercomputer is the “Swiss army knife” of scientific computing, tackling a wide range of research challenges with its 380 petaflop/s of sustained performance powered by state-of-the-art GPU processors. We invite you to explore LUMI’s latest features, real-world use cases, and the transformative potential it offers to researchers and innovators across disciplines. **See you at booth** **H01**!

At the booth, you can also find out how LUMI users can benefit from initiatives like EPICURE and the EuroHPC federation platform (EFP).  Additionally, you can find our experts at the EuroHPC JU speaker’s corner:

- Wednesday 24.6. 14:30–15:00 LUMI & LUMI AI Factory with **Aleksi Kallio**
- Wednesday 24.6. 15:00–15:30 EuroHPC Federation Platform with **Henrik Nortamo**
- Thursday 25.6. 13:00–13:30 EPICURE with **Virvatuli Uusimäki**
