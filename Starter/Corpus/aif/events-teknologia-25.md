# Teknologia 25

![Teknologia25 banner](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Teknologia_1920x1080_ENG-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Place: Helsinki Expo & Convention Center, Helsinki, Finland

Date: 4–6.11.2025

Time: 9:00 – 17:00 EET

Join us at Teknologia 2025 and discover the LUMI AI Factory!

Themed Transforming the Industrial Landscape, Teknologia 2025 is the leading technology and industry event in the Nordics. It showcases the latest smart and sustainable industrial technologies—from automation and digitalization to maintenance and energy solutions. Over three days, the event brings together top companies, experts, and decision-makers to shape the future of industry.

Visit our booth (7d148) to learn how the LUMI AI Factory, one of Europe’s first AI factories, is accelerating AI innovation for companies, the public sector, and researchers. By providing open access to the world-class EuroHPC LUMI supercomputer, high-quality open data, and cutting-edge AI expertise, we enable startups, SMEs, and researchers to build advanced AI models and applications.

LUMI AI Factory Talk Tuesday 4.11. 12:00-12:20, Tech Corner.
“LUMI AI Factory, computing power and services for AI development – free of charge for startups, SMEs, and the public sector”

Come meet us, get inspired, and explore how supercomputing and AI come together with LUMI!
