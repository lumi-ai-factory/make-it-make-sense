## Event details

Location: Kraskov, Czechia

Date: 8.9.2026

Join us at **ISP FutureTec**, the premier gathering for network engineers, tech innovators, and telecommunications leaders. Come visit the **LUMI AI Factory** stand to discover how high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence are shaping the future of next-generation connectivity.

This year’s edition brings a packed agenda focusing on industry-critical challenges, evolving regulations, and physical network boundaries. Expert sessions will cover wireless and fibre networks, traffic configuration, advanced monitoring, and key security vulnerabilities.

Key opportunities available at our stand include:

- **Compute Access:** Direct HPC resources primarily for start-ups and SMEs.
- **Expert Technical Support:** Bespoke consultations for code optimisation, model scaling, and grant application support.
- **Shared Workspaces & Networking:** Access to AI Factory hubs designed to foster talent, collaboration, and industry partnerships.

Integrating the LUMI supercomputer with edge and cloud infrastructure enables real-time data processing—a critical foundation for 6G networks, wireless communication optimisation, robotics, and software-defined vehicles. Stop by our stand to see real-world applications, such as Top Data Science’s collaboration with Nokia (AISA project), and explore how your organisation can leverage supercomputing performance.

Come and visit our booth!
