# Consultation on AI methods

**Expert support for choosing and correctly implementing AI methods, especially deep neural networks, for a specific purpose or use case. The scope of this service is on larger assignments that go beyond typical service desk requests.**

## Service features

AI methods form a toolbox of techniques used to build intelligent systems, ranging from data-driven learning (ML/DL) to natural language processing, computer vision, decision-making, optimization, and rule-based reasoning. If you are unsure which approach best fits your project, we can help you identify the right solution.

Our AI experts provide support in selecting, adapting, and implementing advanced methods for specific research or industrial use cases. The main focus is on deep neural networks and related techniques that require GPU and HPC-level computational resources.

This service is designed for demanding and complex tasks where in-depth consultation, guidance, and collaboration are needed to ensure both methodological soundness and practical effectiveness.

We help you and your team navigate the rapidly evolving AI landscape, ensuring that the chosen approaches are scientifically sound, computationally efficient, and well suited to your target use case.

**Access/Output Requirements**

- There are no requirements for getting this service or reporting results, but if you want to apply the results of the consultation to practice, you need to get access to LUMI. For first-time users we recommend the “[Playground access to LUMI](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/playground-access-to-lumi-ai-factory/)” or “[Try&Buy](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/trybuy/)” (currently available in Finland only).

## Target groups

Startups and SMEs.

## Pricing

The LUMI AI Factory consultation is open and free of charge for startups and SMEs from the EU Member States and countries associated with the Horizon Europe programme.
