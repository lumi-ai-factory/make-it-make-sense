# Finland LUMI AI Factory Hubs

On this page, you can find information on the two LUMI AI Factory Hubs located in Finland,
LUMI AI Factory Hub Otaniemi and LUMI AI Factory Hub Kumpula.

[read more about the LUMI AI Factory Hub policies](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/hubs/)

### At the heart of research and student life, **LUMI AI Factory Hub Otaniemi** welcomes you to join our community.

The hub offers 121 m² of dedicated space, including individual work desks and open areas for meetings, trainings, and events such as workshops, mini-hackathons, and co-working sessions. Book a visit to learn more about LUMI AI Factory expertise and opportunities to develop your AI skills.

For inquiries or visit requests, please contact us through the form below.

#### What we offer

- Courses, trainings, and workshops
- Hackathons
- Study and peer groups (e.g. AI Reading Club, RSE roundtables)
- AI Campus events for students and researchers, including monthly PhD breakfasts
- Domain-specific events, such as *Health Tech Meets AI* (March 2026)
- The premises can be booked for AI-related events or meetings.

### University of Helsinki has opened **LUMI AI Factory Hub Kumpula** to help both researchers and students with their artificial intelligence-related challenges.

There are several premises at the Kumpula campus:

- Various additional offices for AI experts in the Exactum building
- Two shared offices in Exactum 3rd floor A corridor for AI research sofware engineer activities and co-working visitor spaces
- The Hub uses Exactum and Physicum building meeting and lecture rooms of University of Helsinki for organising trainings and hackathons

#### WHAT WE OFFER

- We provide doctoral and postdoctoral researchers with professional AI expert support via online help sessions (see [events calendar](https://tapahtumat.helsinki.fi/en-FI/page/68df95bc281b450007745e56/research-software-engineering-online-help-sessions)).
- We provide research groups with professional AI expert support via collaboration in research projects.
- Kumpula AI Factory Hub is focusing on topics related to AI for Science.

## Do you want to host your own event at the Finland LUMI AI Factory Hubs?

We warmly invite companies, student organizations, and research groups to host events with us. Whether it’s a workshop, seminar, hackathon, meet-and-greet, or a completely new concept, we’re excited to explore creative ideas and develop them together. We’ll work with you to bring your idea to life, using the hubs’ facilities, expertise, and community to make it a success.

Have an idea? Let’s plan together. Request the service via our contact form below, please select “Finland LUMI AI Factory Hubs” from the inquiry type dropdown.
