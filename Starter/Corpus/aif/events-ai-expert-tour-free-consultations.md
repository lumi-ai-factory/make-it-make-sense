# AI Expert Tour – free consultations

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AI-Expert-Tour_960x520.jpg)

**Event details**

Place: Sokos Hotel Tripla (Jakokoski & Vuolenkoski rooms), Helsinki, Finland

Date: 18.11.2026

Time: 12:00–16:00 EET

Official Slush community side event – no Slush ticket required

Attention all AI startups!

Has your promising AI development stalled due to an unexpected bottleneck? Are you hesitating on your next move and want to avoid costly or time consuming mistakes? Do you need more data or increased computing power to advance your work? Or is your AI idea still in the early stages of development and need validation?

We, LUMI AI Factory, are bringing our AI experts to the Sokos Hotel Tripla – right next to Slush – to answer your questions, help resolve bottlenecks, and discuss next steps.

**Sign up for a rapid consultation with our AI experts.** To make the most out of your time, please be prepared to briefly describe the challenge you are facing in your AI development.

- Sign up for the slot between **12:00 and 14:00 if you are in the early stages of your AI journey.**
- Sign up for the slot between **14:00 and 16:00 if you are in the implementation phase of your AI journey.**

LUMI AI Factory is one of the 19 AI factories in Europe. We aim to empower European AI startups to develop innovative AI models and applications through a comprehensive and accessible service infrastructure.
