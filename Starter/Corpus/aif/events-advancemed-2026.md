# AdvanceMed 2026

![Advance_Med2026](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AdvanceMed2026_LAIF-1.png)

## Event details

Place: Lower Vitkovice, Multifunctional auditorum Gong, Czechia

Date: 9.–10.4.2026

Time:
THU 8:30 – 16:45
FRI 8:30 – 15:40

Come and meet us at the second annual AdvanceMed conference, which will focus on the latest trends and opportunities in AI, VR, robotics, telemedicine and other modern technologies in healthcare.

This conference focuses on integrating AI, VR, and robotics across healthcare sectors, including radiology, neurology, and cardiology. It serves as a key meeting point for medical specialists, management, and tech innovators to discuss the latest trends in diagnostics and treatment.

You can find us at the IT4Innovations stand, where we will be showcasing LUMI AI Factory services with the goal to provide healthcare professionals and startups with the high-performance infrastructure needed to scale their medical AI projects.
