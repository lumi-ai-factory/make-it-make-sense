# Cerna.AI Festival

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Cerna.ai_.festival.it4i-960x540.png)

## Event details

Location: Multifunctional Auditorium Gong, Ruska 2993, 703 00 Ostrava 3, Czechia

Date: 15.9.2026

Time: 7:30–23:00 CEST

CERNA.AI Festival is an AI gathering where technology meets development, creative arts, industrial applications, and everyday life. Designed for industry professionals, businesses, households, and tech enthusiasts alike, the festival offers a unique programme.

- **7 stages** equipped with high-definition LED screens
- **60+ leading experts** in artificial intelligence and IT
- **5+ artistic performances**, including a live concert
- **12+ interactive AI workshops & hackathons**
- **Comprehensive AI Expo** tailored for both corporate enterprises and home everyday use

### Featured Presence: IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center & LUMI AI Factory

IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center and LUMI AI Factory are serving as official co-organisers and expert partners of this year’s CERNA.AI Festival. Bringing world-class supercomputing expertise and practical AI implementation directly to the festival, our contributions include:

- **LUMI AI Factory stage:** A dedicated stage featuring inspiring keynote speakers and expert discussions focused on practical AI adoption and cutting-edge technology integration.
- **Specialised workshop:** *“Run Your Own AI Models on a Supercomputer Without Technical Barriers”* – Discover the AI Training App built on the LEXIS platform, designed to eliminate high hardware demands and the need for complex technical expertise.
- **LUMI AI Factory stand:** Visit our stand where we will demonstrate how we link supercomputing processing power, data services, and expert AI consultancy. Learn how startups, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), industrial leaders, public institutions, and research organisations can easily access these high-performance services.
