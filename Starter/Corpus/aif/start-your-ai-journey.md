# Start Your AI Journey

*Compute, data, or training? Identify the right direction and kickstart your project with expert guidance from our specialists.*

## Service features

This service is a primary entry point for companies and institutions looking to leverage AI and supercomputing. Key features include:

- **Needs analysis:** Identification of technical and business requirements for your project.
- **Flexible approach:** Options for either an AI self-assessment survey or a direct consultation with the LUMI AI Factory team

No specific technical preparation is required. A general idea of the your company’s current state of AI preparedness or goal you wish to address is sufficient.

## Target groups

Startups, SMEs, large enterprises, research institutions, universities, and public administration.

## Request process

**Option 1**: Fill the ‘[AI self-assessment survey](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/ai-self-assessment-survey/)‘. Based on your responses, a Customer Manager will contact you and help you with the next steps of your AI journey, e.g., to guide you towards specific experts and/or services.

Option 2: Just fill our Contact form below and select the Inquiry type ‘Start Your AI Journey’. A Customer Manager will contact you and help you with the next steps of your AI journey.

## Pricing

The LUMI AI Factory consultation is open and free of charge for startups, SMEs, research institutions, universities and public administration from the EU Member States and countries associated with the Horizon Europe programme.
