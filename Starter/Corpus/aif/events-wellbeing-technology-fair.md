# Wellbeing Technology Fair

![Hyvinvointiteknologia messut](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/HTM.jpg)

## Event details

Place: Pori, Finland

Date: 28.1.2026

Time: 9:00-16:00 EET

Discover the latest in wellness and health technology!

The Wellbeing Technology Fair (Hyvinvointiteknologiamessut) showcases a wide range of innovative technologies and digital services from leading exhibitors.

LUMI AI Factory will be on-site to share how our solutions can transform the health sector.

The event will be held on 28.1.2026 in Pori at Messukeskus Taitava in Teljäntori (Liisankatu 7) and Promenadikeskus (Yrjönkatu 17). Participation in the event is free of charge.
