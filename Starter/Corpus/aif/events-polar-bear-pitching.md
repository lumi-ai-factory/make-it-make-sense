# Polar Bear Pitching

![Polar Bear Pitching](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PBP.png)

## Event details

Place: Oulu, Finland

Date: 26.2.2026

Day Conference
9:00–15:00 EET

Avanto Competition
17:00–20:00 EET

Join us at Polar Bear Pitching, where startups showcase their resilience and commitment by pitching from an unconventional stage: Avanto (Finnish for “ice hole”).

Polar Bear Pitching Avanto Competition, an exciting outdoor event broadcasted worldwide, will be held at the Oulu Market Square, where you will also find the Sauna Village – come and meet us there! Visit the LUMI AI Factory booth and discover how we can support your startup, free of charge even!

You can also learn more about the services we offer during the Day Conference, held at Radisson Blu, Oulu. Join our “LUMI AI Factory – How we support startups” session at the Startup Studio workshops at 12:30-12:55 EET.
