# InnoVerse 2025

![Innoverse Czechia Event, November 27th, 2025](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Innoverse_2025_event-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Place:  DK Poklad, Ostrava – Poruba, Czechia

Date: 27.11.2025

Quantum, Supercomputing, and AI Factories at InnoVerse 2025

The primary barrier preventing businesses from using quantum technologies is a **lack of information** regarding their practical application in data processing and complex problem-solving. **This similar lack of understanding often applies to how companies can best utilise the power of supercomputers and AI Factories.**

To find clarity, visit the General Partners’ stand at InnoVerse 2025: **IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center, EuroCC Czechia, and LUMI AI Factory**. We will provide essential insights into:

- The practical use of quantum computing (including the Czech **VLQ** computer) and how supercomputing power fits into complex industrial tasks.
- you will learn about the services offered by the **AI Factories** and what specific services your company can utilise right now.
- Additionally, discover how your business can gain **free access** to European high-performance supercomputing and quantum capacities.

Join us on **27 November 2025** at DK Poklad, Ostrava, Czechia. Find us in the foyer.
