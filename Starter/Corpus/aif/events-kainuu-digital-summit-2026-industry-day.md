# Kainuu Digital Summit 2026 – Industry Day

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KDS2026.png)

## Event details

Location: Kaukametsä Conference Centre, Kajaani, Finland

Date: 5.11.2026

Join us at Kainuu Digital Summit and discover how companies can create real business value with AI.

The day is dedicated to industrial AI solutions and real implementations: what companies have built, how they deployed it, and future directions. You will hear directly from organisations that have made AI and their data work. Meet the people and enter the ecosystem that can help you do the same.

During the summit, Managing Director of CSC **Kimmo Koski** will be giving a presentation on the LUMI AI Factory. You can also visit our booth – come and discover how we can support each stage of your AI development with the services we offer, free of charge for SMEs and startups!

**[Sign up also to our AI Expert Tour consulting sessions](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/events/ai-expert-tour-in-kajaani/)**, where our experts will answer your questions, help you tackle bottlenecks, and discuss the next steps in your AI development work.

KDS2026 Industry Day is organised by the [AIKA Ecosystem](https://aikaecosystem.com) in co-operation with LUMI AI Factory.
