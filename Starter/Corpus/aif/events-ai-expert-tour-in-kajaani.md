# AI Expert Tour – Kajaani

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AI-Expert-Tour_960x520.jpg)

Event details

Place: Congress and Cultural Centre Kaukametsä, Kajaani, Finland

Date: 5.11.2026

Time: 13:00–17:00 EET

Attention companies in the Kainuu region interested in AI – our experts are coming to Kajaani in November!

Has your promising AI development stalled due to an unexpected bottleneck? Are you hesitating on your next move and want to avoid costly or time consuming mistakes? Do you need more data or computing power to advance your work? Or is your AI idea still in the early stages of development and need validation?

The LUMI AI Factory, together with the [AIKA Ecosystem](https://aikaecosystem.com), is bringing AI experts to Kajaani to answer your questions, help you tackle bottlenecks, and discuss the next steps in your development work. The event will be held in conjunction with the [Kainuu Digital Summit](https://aikaecosystem.com/kainuu-digital-summit-2026-industry-day-5th-nov/) in Meeting Room 308 at the Kaukametsä Congress and Culture Centre.

To get the most out of the event, please be prepared to briefly describe the challenge you have encountered in your AI development work.

- Register for the 13:00–15:00 session if your AI development work is still at an early stage.
- Register for the 15:00–17:00 session if your AI development work has already reached the implementation stage.
