# Is your data ready for AI and HPC?

## Event details

Location: Online

Date: 26.11.2026

Time: 10:00–11:30 EET

This webinar provides a practical overview of AI-ready data, data formats for HPC and AI, and LUMI AI Factory data services, highlighting how good data practices support efficient and responsible AI development in industry and research.

AI and HPC place increasing demands on data. An AI pilot works well with a small local dataset, but scaling it to larger volumes of data requires different formats, storage approaches, and access methods. Well-managed, well-documented, and accessible data supports data reuse, computational workflows, and the effective use of AI and computing services across research and industry. This helps organisations get more value from existing datasets while reducing the time spent finding, preparing, and reproducing data for new AI projects.

This webinar provides a practical overview of key topics, including AI-ready data, data formats for HPC and AI, and LUMI AI Factory data services that support secure and responsible access to valuable datasets for AI development. The session brings together perspectives from both data providers and data users, highlighting how data management practices influence data sharing, accessibility, and reuse.

Welcome to learn how good data practices can help you get more value from data and computing resources!

## Target audience

Anyone working with data, AI, HPC, research, development and innovation (RDI), including researchers, data stewards, data managers, SMEs, startups, and innovation teams seeking to make their data AI-ready.

## Agenda

- **Introduction to data management and AI-ready data**
  Discover what makes data AI-ready and why good data management is critical for successful AI projects.
- **Data formats and best practices for HPC and AI**
  Explore data formats and practical approaches that improve performance, scalability, and interoperability in AI and HPC workflows.
- **LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service: restricted datasets and data provider processes**
  Discover how organisations can make confidential datasets available for AI and HPC use while maintaining governance, fostering collaboration, and unlocking new value from their data.
- **AI-ready data in practice: lessons from a real dataset**
  Using a dataset from Ekoälyä project as an example, discover how good data management practices enable AI use.
- **Q&A**
