# Researchers’ Night at IT4Innovation National Computing Center

## Event details

Location: IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center,
Studentska 6231/1B
708 00 Ostrava,
Czechia

Date: 25.9.2026

Time: 17:00–22:00 CEST

Science is a job like any other! On Friday, 25th September, the IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Centre will be transformed into a vibrant place filled with hands-on experiences, discovery, and entertainment for the whole family.

Ever wondered what goes on behind the scenes of cutting-edge scientific research? Science isn’t just about formulas and textbooks – it’s an exciting, dynamic job like any other. Once a year, thanks to Researchers’ Night, you have a unique opportunity to take an exclusive look behind the scenes.

Key opportunities available at our stand include:

- **Explore supercomputing:** Peek inside the home of the most powerful supercomputers in the Czech Republic.
- **Quantum technology:** See the very first Czech quantum computer up close.
- **Talk to experts:** Engage with AI specialists and discover technologies shaping our future.
- **Interactive fun:** Enjoy playful experiments, interactive demonstrations, workshops, and short talks tailored for all ages.
