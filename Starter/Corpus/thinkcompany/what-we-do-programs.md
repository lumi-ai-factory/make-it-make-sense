# Programmes

## From thinking to action

Idea competitions, challenges and accelerator programmes on a multitude of topics – something for everyone. Get ready to develop your problem-solving skills, get creative and put your knowledge into action!

## **Did you know that...**

![An illustration of a free ticket](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c9d2c1383d819de5365628_kuvitus%20programs1.png)

Participation is **always free!** On top of that, we often reward the best ideas at the end of each programme.

![An illustration of three people waving](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c9d2ba30126f0fa4d01a36_kuvitus%20programs2.png)

We have programmes for both **team and individual applicants**. Individuals will be matched with others, to create new teams.

![An illustration of "raise the roof" hands](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c9d2b65d49c70762661242_kuvitus%20programs3.png)

**You don’t need experience to get experience**. When it comes to getting accepted to our programmes, it’s your motivation what counts the most.

## Think Company Fund

**When & where:**

Pitching final on 11 November at Think Corner

**Application:**

Now open until 25 October 2026

**Language:**

English

**Open for:**

Early-stage companies and teams

With the Think Company Fund, we support promising early-stage companies that aim to create a positive impact. Apply for a grant to launch or grow your business, advance to the pitching final, and compete for your share of the funding. In 2026, a total of €6,000 will be awarded. Since 2015, the Think Company Fund has provided over €100,000 to support student entrepreneurship.

[Read more](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/think-company-fund)

## HELSEED workshops

**When & where:**

April 2026, Terkko Health Hub

**Application:**

Now open

**Language:**

English

**Open for:**

Students, researchers, entrepreneurs and teams

To break down barriers to entrepreneurship, we proudly present to you: HELSEED workshop series, autumn 2026 edition. Delivered with a logical progression, these workshops contain all the things you need to consider when taking your business idea to the next level. The workshops are organised in collaboration with the University of Helsinki, as a part of the [HELSEED funding](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/strategy-economy-and-quality/university-finance/helseed).

### HELSEED workshops

#### since 2020

Organised together with the University of Helsinki, this entrepreneurship program encourages students to turn their ideas into companies. Early-stage funding of up to 50,000 euros is offered to the most promising ideas.

#### since 2017

Programme where researchers, politicians & students come together to solve problems identified by scientific research. Intention is to build bridges between policymaking & research, challenging traditional decision-making.

## Highlights from past programmes

![CASSINI Hackathon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/68bee30fcbf5b28a5e5134d9_Copy%20of%20CASSINI_Hackathon%2010_1920x1080%20general%20info.png)

### CASSINI Hackathon

#### 2025

Think Company was the local organiser of the 10th CASSINI Hackathon, that took place in 10 different locations across Europe simultaneously. During the challenge weekend, teams utilised EU space data to create innovative solutions tailored for consumers. They could choose between challenges in retail, gaming and travel.

![ENVISION](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/64896f3c94f78eea79fa8d64_ENVISION%20Twitter%20launch.jpg)

### ENVISION

#### 2023

ENVISION was a design thinking mini-programme for doctoral researchers of the University. Participants developed concrete problems and solutions related to either Sustainable Everyday Life or Equal Opportunities.

![Nest Hackathon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/6455208f986b26851ec8f971_Nest%202023%20banner%20WIP.png)

### Nest Hackathon

#### 2023

Nest Hackathon was a weekend challenge competition where professionals from different fields collaborated to create gamified solutions for sustainable housing. Nest was developed in partnership with DeCarbon-Home.

![Hack Sote](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/640f4aa2c0d824c5b17fb5d6_Hack%20Sote%20banneri%202023%20(3).png)

### Hack Sote

#### 2023

Hack Sote oli haasteviikonloppu, jossa sosiaali- ja terveysalan opiskelijat yhdistivät osaamisensa paremman sote-alan tulevaisuuden puolesta.

![SÄRÖ/FRACTURE](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/6318f1056ddc2827c2992b6f_Saro_Fracture%20brandikuva.jpg)

### SÄRÖ/FRACTURE

#### 2022—2023

SÄRÖ/FRACTURE was a pre-incubator for those who want to change society and its institutions for the better and investigate just how great of an impact even the smallest of ideas can have. Think Company was organising the programme in collaboration with Helsinki Incubators.

![Circular Economy Sprint](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/636bc94c528dc70edf4a29fe_Circular%20Economy%20Sprint_banner.jpg)

### Circular Economy Sprint

#### 2022

In the Circular Economy Sprint participants had a chance to dive into the world of green entrepreneurship. The programme focused on endorsing sustainable systemic change by encouraging new green business models.

![Gam-e-quality](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/628e06fbe464ea211cf73e3e_Gam-e-quality%20banner%20(1).png)

### Gam-e-quality

#### 2022

Gam-e-quality was a hackathon where teams created solutions to inclusivity and accessibility challenges in the game industry and gaming communities.

![Una.Together](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/6233531f89aa6a838a424fff_program.png)

### Una.Together

#### 2022

Una.Together was a seven-week online programme that brought together students from all over Europe. The programme seeked solutions to advance diversity, equity and inclusion in academic communities. Each Una Europa university introduced a current challenge that an international group of students created a solution for. The programme consisted of lectures, workshops and a final event streamed across Europe.

![Kollaasi ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/6225c0b7bbb228423932b6fc_kollaasi_thumbnail.jpg)

### Kollaasi

#### 2022

Kollaasi was a weekend programme where people interested in the future of culture and art industries were brought together to create solutions to the structural challenges in the field. Teams tackled the issues with sustainable and creative practical solutions. Kollaasi was co-organized with The University of the Arts Helsinki.

![Terkko Health X](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/62b9af4480027308a6647b07_2)%20Terkko%20Health%20X%20slogan.png)

### Terkko Health X

#### 2019—2023

Terkko Health X was a pre-incubator programme for health and life sciences targeted towards early stage teams with ambition to create impactful solutions that solve real healthcare problems. The programme provided the tools and experts to take teams' health and life science concepts into the market. Terkko Health X was co-organized by Terkko Health Hub.

![Robot Uprising](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/60be6737c37274853ffae668_robot_uprising_uusi.jpg)

### Robot Uprising

#### 2017—

Europe's biggest hands-on robotics challenge where gaming, puzzles and storytelling are all combined. Robot Uprising brings together people to programme, design and build robots to compete in epic arenas. Started from community members' initiative, Robot Uprising has later spun out to become its own volunteer-led community.

![Mutiny](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6047587deb9d01d66d5a91c2/60be67aa2d0cbe6faf30ea82_mutiny%20logo.jpg)

### Mutiny

#### 2018—2020

Mutiny was an accelerator programme for early-stage teams with a socially or environmentally impactful business idea. A chance to find mentors, funding, and break through!
