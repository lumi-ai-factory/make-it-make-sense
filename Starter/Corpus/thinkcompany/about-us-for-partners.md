## What is Helsinki Think Company

Helsinki Think Company is the entrepreneurship community of the University of Helsinki. Our mission is to turn academics into concrete action and foster impactful entrepreneurship by organising innovation programmes, idea accelerators, hackathons, and workshops around universal phenomena that have an impact on the world on an environmental or societal level.

We bring together the industry and academia. We collaborate with corporations, startups, research institutions, cities, and ministries to tackle environmental and societal challenges.

We are an active and diverse community, consisting of entrepreneurs, researchers, students, and businesses — many of which were born from our programmes. For our community, we offer spaces, innovation programmes, and entrepreneurial support & sparring.

In short, Helsinki Think Company has accelerated science-based multidisciplinary solutions for the past 10 years — from vegan ice cream to cancer treatments.

## What we offer

Solve your challenges with our innovation programmes

### 4UNI solution competition

- Set a challenge for our flagship program
- Multidisciplinary teams create solutions to the challenge in a 7-week long program

### Challenge Weekend

- 3-day long open innovation challenge
- Set a challenge for multidisciplinary teams to solve
- Facilitated co-creation with diverse teams of students, researchers, and experts
- Design Thinking based facilitated problem-solving

- Pitch coaching & workshops for leaders, entrepreneurs, and scientists
- Design thinking problem-solving workshops

## Coworking spaces

We offer communal coworking spaces at each of the University of Helsinki campuses. Our spaces are open to all people wherever they are along their entrepreneurial path. We have all the basic stuff you need in an office – free of charge. With a membership, you can also unlock meeting rooms and personal desks at our Meilahti location.
