# Introducing Helsinki Think Company's Knowledge Bank

Startup ecosystem and entrepreneurs, hear this! We have discovered a new problem in our midst: Around the web, there is too much scattered information on entrepreneurship. Would you agree?

By

Lahja Vierimaa

Eeva Siika-aho

January 26, 2022

Happy New Year from the Helsinki Think Company team! We’re kicking off the year with an exciting launch. We at Helsinki Think Company have created a Knowledge Bank for entrepreneurship, to support putting academic skills to action and simplifying information retrieval on entrepreneurship.

## Introducing Knowledge Bank

For almost a decade we at Helsinki Think Company have gathered resources, insight and contacts of the entrepreneurship ecosystem. Unfortunately during COVID and remote working, this expertise and knowledge has not been so tangible to our community members. As much as we’ve seen this as being a major problem for many individuals and teams who need our support, we have also recognised that there might be too much information out there scattered around the web. News, courses, events, accelerator programs, incubators, you name it – you can have it all! Turnside to all of this abundance is that it is creating information overload, which can cause exhaustion and confusion amongst the very people we are trying to help. Therefore, we have worked vigorously for the last couple of months to bring everything we know together for an easily accessible, 24/7 entrepreneurial support feature on our website. Whether you are a student, a researcher or an entrepreneur wishing to know how to make the best of your skills, we are providing a one stop shop for all resources around you to take a look at.

## [Dive in!](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/knowledge-bank)

As information tends to change, we highly encourage YOU to take part in developing this concept! Is there something missing? What information do you need more of? We are looking to develop and update the bank as often as we can, and you are welcome to send your suggestions and questions to [support@thinkcompany.fi](mailto:support@thinkcompany.fi)!
