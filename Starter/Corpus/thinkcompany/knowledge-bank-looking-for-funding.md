## Looking for funding

Get to know early stage investors and apply for benefits as soon as possible!

## [**Think Company Fund**](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/think-company-fund) **- supporting promising early stage teams**

Helsinki Think Company supports promising early-stage teams that aim to make a positive impact on our world and lives! Think Fund is a grant for innovative teams that want to put their ideas into action. You can apply for a grant of 2500 euros (max. 850 € / person) to get your business up and going, or for a grant of 500 euros to test and validate your business idea. Find out more about the grant and application process.

[Think Company Fund](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/think-company-fund-2021)

## [**HELSEED**](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/innovations-and-cooperation/innovations-and-entrepreneurship/incubator-and-entrepreneurship-services/helseed-startup-programme) **– Encouraging students on their entrepreneurial paths**

HELSEED is a workshop series co-organized by Helsinki Think Company and University of Helsinki. Delivered with a logical progression, HELSEED workshops contain all the things one needs to consider when setting up shop. One can pick and choose which workshops fit one’s needs the best, or attend the whole series. Participation in the programme does not require participants to set up a business or submit a funding application. Find out more information on HELSEED.

[HELSEED](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/strategy-economy-and-quality/university-finance/helseed)

### [**Pay subsidy**](https://tem.fi/en/pay-subsidy)

A pay subsidy is an economic benefit that a TE Office may grant an employer to cover pay costs of an unemployed jobseeker. The purpose of work supported by pay subsidy is to enhance the professional competence of prospective employees to be hired with pay subsidy, and to help them find work in the open labour market. Read more about pay subsidy.

[Pay subsidy](https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/en/more-information-about-services/pay-subsidy)

## [**Tesi (Finnish Industry Investment Ltd)**](https://tesi.fi/en/for-companies/)

Tesi is a state-owned investment company that invests in companies with genuine potential for global scalable growth and a proven ability to expand. Tesi handles all state-led investments in Finland after merging with Business Finland Venture Capital Ltd in 2024.

[Tesi](https://tesi.fi/en/for-companies/)

### [**Finnish Business Angels Network**](https://fiban.org/)

FIBAN is a business angel network that focuses on linking high-growth companies to their investor members for free. FIBAN offers visibility, business angel contacts and support in seeking funding and pitching to their business angel network. Read more about FIBAN’s services.

[FIBAN](https://fiban.org/)

### [**Voima Ventures**](https://voimaventures.com/)

Voima Ventures helps founders to accelerate the growth of deep technology ventures to global markets. Voima Ventures sheds light on the ideas and technology which require time and courage to build. Their core idea is to combine science-driven innovation together with the Nordic serial entrepreneurial experience and a global mindset. Read more about Voima Ventures.

[Voima Ventures](https://voimaventures.com/)
