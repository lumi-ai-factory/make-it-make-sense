# Startup stories I Digital Wellbeing Event Series: Changemakers

In this Digital Wellbeing event, held 19th of May, we introduce you to entrepreneurial possibilities and trends in the field of digital wellbeing business. You will hear stories from startup entrepreneurs, how they came up with their ideas and how they see the future of wellbeing business. We approach well-being business from many perspectives - team perspective, physical health and mental health.
