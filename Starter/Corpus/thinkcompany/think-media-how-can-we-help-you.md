# How can we help you? Answer a short questionnaire about our services

Helsinki Think Company currently offers a variety of services including workshops, coworking spaces and mentoring, but we are hoping to expand the support and tools we provide to our community members. Please give us your opinion on the services you would like us to develop!

By

Laura Haapala

October 11, 2022

Dear community members and aspiring entrepreneurs,

This autumn we have finally been able to fully return to the university campuses, and have been delighted to see so many familiar and new faces around! After two years of online content, we are now back in business with our coworking spaces and live programs and events.

In addition to reviving our old activities, we are looking to further improve the services we provide. Whether you are an active community member or just curious about starting your own business, we would love to hear your thoughts on our services! Please answer our questionnaire to help us support you event better in the future.
