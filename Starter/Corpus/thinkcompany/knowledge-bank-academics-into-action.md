## Academics into action

Do you want to turn your academic skills into business? Look into these incubators and programmes organised by Helsinki Think Company & University of Helsinki.

## **ENTREPRENEURIAL PROGRAMMES**

### [HELSEED](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

Entrepreneurship programme for students to develop their product and service ideas into business

### [PsyHack](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/psyhack)

Multidisciplinary programme for psychology related service and product ideas

### [Pathways by Helsinki Incubators](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/networks/helsinki-incubators/pre-incubators/pathways-general-pre-incubator)

Interdisciplinary early-stage pre-incubator programme
