## Inspiration, news & events

Do you want to learn something new and get inspired about entrepreneurship? We’ve put together events, sites, and content to accelerate your thinking.

## EVENTS

## [**Volunteering at Slush**](https://slush.org/volunteers/)

Slush is a not-for-profit organisation and the world’s leading startup event. Held annually in Helsinki, Slush is built by volunteer teams. Volunteering is a great way to gain work experience and startup knowledge, get inspired by the speeches of tech professionals, and have fun. Find out more about Slush.

[Slush Helsinki](https://slush.org/audience/volunteers)

## [Arctic15](https://arctic15.com/)

Arctic15 is a 2-day matchmaking startup event organized in Northern Europe. The event brings together international startups, investors, companies, media, and influencers. The event is focused on various industries, technologies, and topics, such as Digital Health, AI, Social Impact and Scaling. Read more about the event and its offering.

[Arctic15](https://arctic15.com/)

## **MEDIA**

### [**Think Media**](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/think-media)

Helsinki Think Company's Think Media blog content offers a comprehensive overview of the core topics of the entrepreneurial community. Get to know the themes of entrepreneurship through entrepreneurial stories, programme content and current social events. Expand your thinking and read more about Think Media.

[Think Media](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/think-media)

### [**Medium**](https://medium.com/)

Medium is an open platform where readers can discover new ideas and where experts of different fields can share their writing on any topic. Medium offers monthly and annual memberships to get unlimited access to the platform's content, write and connect with other like-minded users. Red more about Mediums content and memberships:

[Medium](https://medium.com/)

### [**Slush Newsroom**](https://slush.org/newsroom/)

Slush Newsroom is a digital media that offers stories related to Slush, the startup scene and building companies as well as fresh insights into European technology and data-based observations of the world around us. Check out Newsroom's extensive articles.

[Slush Newsroom](https://slush.org/newsroom)

### [**Bonfire**](https://bonfire.fi/)

Bonfire is a digital business media where Finnish business professionals produce current content in the form of articles, videos, and podcasts. The contents cover key business themes such as management, marketing, finance, HR, ICT, digitalisation, and entrepreneurship. Check out Bonfire's diverse content in more detail:

[Bonfire.fi](https://www.bonfire.fi/)
