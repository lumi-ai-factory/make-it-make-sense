How does the AI Act work? 📜

the common principles, concisely

The EU AI Act (2024) sorts AI by risk: the more harm a system could cause to people, the stricter the rules it must follow.

  • 🚫 Banned — practices that go against EU values (manipulating people, exploiting vulnerabilities, social scoring) may never be used at all.
  • ⚠️ High-risk — AI that decides or strongly influences important things about people (jobs, credit, exams, emergencies) must meet strict requirements before it may be used.
  • 🤖 Must be transparent — chatbots must say they are AI, and AI-made images, text or video must be identifiable as AI.
  • ✅ Basically fine — everything else can be built and shipped without new AI-specific rules.

Breaking the Act can cost up to €35 million or 7% of global turnover. It has been in force since August 2024, phasing in until 2027.

Concise, not exhaustive — and not legal advice. The full text: artificialintelligenceact.eu.
