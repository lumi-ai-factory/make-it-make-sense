# HealthTech meets AI

## Event details

Place: LUMI AI Factory Hub Otaniemi, Aalto University

Maarintie 8, Espoo, 4th floor, 02150 Espoo

Date: 31.3.2026

Time: 14:00 – 16:00 EEST

HealthTech meets AI – networking event at LUMI AI Factory Hub in Otaniemi

Join us for an inspiring afternoon where cutting‑edge **health technology** meets the power of **AI and supercomputing**. The LUMI AI Factory Hub in Otaniemi invites startups, SMEs and innovators to explore how Finland’s top-tier AI ecosystem can accelerate health-related RDI work. Are you developing AI solutions for **diagnostics, drug discovery, biomedical imaging, medical software, LLMs/GenAI**, or **medical devices**?

Come discover how your company can benefit from **free AI resources, supercomputing capacity, and expert support**. Did you know that EuroHPC LUMI — one of the world’s most powerful AI and supercomputing platforms — is available for Finnish companies’ RDI work? LUMI AI Factory especially supports startups and SMEs in harnessing AI, from GenAI solutions to advanced AI modelling. Let’s explore how these capabilities could lift your development to the next level.

## What you will learn

**AI trends in healthcare**:
**Jiancheng (JC) Yang**, Principal Investigator at the ELLIS Institute Finland and Assistant Professor at Aalto University, will share current research directions and opportunities in AI for health. Yang leads the Health Intelligence (HINT) research group, focusing on:

- AI for clinical decision support
- Medical image analysis
- 3D vision and spatial intelligence
- Gnerative AI and multimodal learning
- Foundation models for lung cancer and cardiac disease diagnostics

He is widely known for developing MedMNIST, has authored over 60 publications, and brings experience as a medical AI startup co-founder.

**Startup case: Gosta Labs**
Hear how this Otaniemi-based healthtech startup has leveraged the massive AI capacity of LUMI to develop their healthcare AI assistant—giving a strong boost to their RDI work across Europe. Ecosystem services that boost AI innovation

**Short presentations from:**

- City of Espoo
- FAIR – Finnish AI Region EDIH
- AI Finland
- LUMI AI Factory

You’ll hear concrete ways your company can join regional, national, and European AI development initiatives.
