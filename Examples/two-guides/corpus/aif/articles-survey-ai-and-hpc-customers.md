# Participate in a survey about AI and HPC customer needs!

1 min read

12.11.2025

 ![We want to hear from you!](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/SoMe_1200X630_v2-960x540.jpg)

We invite you to participate in a short survey that supports the development of future AI and High-Performance Computing (HPC) services under the LUMI AI Factory initiative.

This survey targets organisations across sectors, including start-ups, SMEs, large enterprises, public administrations, academia and research institutions that currently use or plan to explore AI and HPC solutions. Your input will help us assess existing capabilities, identify unmet needs, and shape service offerings tailored to the evolving demands across the LUMI AI consortium countries.

Estimated time to complete: max. 10 minutes

Survey deadline: **December 15, 2025**

Your participation is vital to ensure we represent a wide range of perspectives and regional needs. Please feel free to share the survey with relevant colleagues or partner organisations. If you wish to receive a summary of the survey results later, you may submit your email address at the end of the survey.

[Fill out the survey here](https://iq06aao4pkb.typeform.com/to/KeXYVpJy?typeform-source=www.lumi-supercomputer.eu)

Thank you for your time in advance!
