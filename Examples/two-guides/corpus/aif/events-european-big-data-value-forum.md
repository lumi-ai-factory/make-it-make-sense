# European Big Data Value Forum

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/data_value.png)

## Event details

Location: Galway, Ireland

Date: 30.9.–1.10.2026

Join us at the European Big Data Value Forum!

The European Big Data Value Forum (EBDVF) brings together industry professionals, business developers, researchers and policy-makers from all over Europe and other regions of the world to advance policy actions and industrial and research activities in the areas of data and AI.

On Thursday 1 October at 14:00–15:00, the LUMI AI Factory will be holding an interactive workshop “**Accelerating European AI Innovation: Connecting AI Factories, Industry, and Digital Ecosystems**“, where you will discover our service offering, real world industry perspectives and emerging AI use cases, as well as benefits offered by tight collaboration between European AI infrastructures.

Whether you are an experienced AI innovator seeking access to data and computing resources, an SME or a startup exploring AI opportunities, or an EDIH or a TEF looking to partner up with AI factories, this workshop is for you.
