# Think compliance first: Lessons from a first-time PsyHack mentor

1 min read

2.6.2026

 ![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Joseph-8191_2000x1000-1024x512-1-960x512.jpg)

Joseph Mugumya joined PsyHack 2026 as a first-time mentor to help the teams realise their ideas. As a Junior Machine Learning Specialist at LUMI AI Factory, he brought hands-on expertise to all questions dealing with AI governance and data.

LUMI AI Factory was the official partner for PsyHack 2026, which was organised 10-12 April 2026 at Terkko Health Hub, Helsinki. After the event, Joseph sat down with **Eveliina Holopainen** to reflect on his experience working with the teams and how LUMI AI Factory can help hackathon participants take their ideas forward.

Read the full interview on [Helsinki Think Company’s website](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/think-media/think-compliance-first-lessons-from-a-first-time-psyhack-mentor).
