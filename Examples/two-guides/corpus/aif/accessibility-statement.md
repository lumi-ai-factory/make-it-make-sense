# Accessibility statement [[copied from here](https://www.lumi-supercomputer.eu/accessibility/)]

This accessibility statement applies to https://lumi-supercomputer.eu/ and was updated on 09.01.2025.

The service is subject to the Act on the provision of digital services 306/2019, which requires that public online services must be accessible.

The accessibility of the service has been evaluated by an external organization.

## Compliance status

Does not meet all critical accessibility requirements.

## Non-accessible content

The website is not yet fully compliant with requirements (WCAG 2.1).

### Perceivable: Missing text-alternatives

#### Non-accessible content and its shortcomings

There are non-text content with missing text alternatives. Images added without alt text, will be added with empty alt-attribute, indicating ”decorative image”.

#### Accessibility requirements that are not met

1.1.1 Non-text Content

## Did you notice an accessibility issue in our digital service? Let us know and we will do our best to correct the shortcoming

### By email

Please send us an email to webmaster@csc.fi

## Supervision authority

If you notice accessibility problems on the website, start by giving feedback to us, that is, the website administrator. Receiving a response may take 14 days. If you are not satisfied with the response from us or if you do not receive any response within two weeks, you may file a report with the [Regional State Administrative Agency for Southern Finland](https://www.saavutettavuusvaatimukset.fi/kayttajan-oikeudet/) (opens in new window). The agency website has detailed instructions (in Finnish and Swedish only) on how to file a complaint and how the issue will be processed.

## We are constantly working to improve accessibility

### We are committed to improve the accessibility of our digital services

We will update this statement as we correct the deficiencies. The goal has been set that the accessibility deficiencies have been corrected by 31.12.2024. Regarding to the deficiencies related to the content management system, we update the accessibility deficiencies when the software supplier makes the improvements.

We are committed improve the accessibility of our services. We ensure accessibility with, among other things, the following measures:

- We develop the site’s accessibility in the first phase by prioritizing the repair order based on the accessibility audit report
- We take accessibility requirements into account when we develop services.
- We already take accessibility requirements into account when we make purchases.
- We support our staff in producing accessible content
