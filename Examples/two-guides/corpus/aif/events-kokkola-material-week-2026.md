# Kokkola Material Week 2026

![Kokkola Material Week 2026 Event page](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Untitled-design-2-960x540.png)

## Event details

**Date**: 9.–13.11.2026

**Location**: Kokkolasali of Kokkola City Hall (Kauppatori 5, Kokkola, Finland)

Meet LUMI AI Factory at Kokkola Material Week 2026!

Curious about how AI could support your business, research, or innovation activities? Visit the LUMI AI Factory stand at Kokkola Material Week 2026 to discuss your ideas, challenges, and opportunities with our experts.

Whether you’re taking your first steps with AI or looking to scale existing projects, we can help you identify the right resources, expertise, and pathways to move forward.

On 10 November at 13:25, our Customer Manager **Morthen Mathisen** will be giving a presentation on the LUMI supercomputer and AI services. Alongside him, AI Solutions Architect **Eliecer Díaz** from Brillian Oy will be speaking about CAD geometry generation for manufacturing with AI.

You can also find us at the exhibitor area in the Gamlakarleby Lounge at Kokkola City Hall on 10-11 November.

**Stop by our stand and let’s explore what AI could do for your organisation.**
