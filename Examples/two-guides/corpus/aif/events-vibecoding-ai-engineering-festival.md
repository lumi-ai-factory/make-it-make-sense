# Vibecoding & AI Engineering Festival

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/LUMI-AI-Factory-Hub-Ostrava-1-1-960x540.png)

## Event details

Location:
LUMI AI Factory Hub,
VIVA Building, 3rd floor
Technologicka 375/5, 708 00
Ostrava, Czechia

Date: 26.11.2026

The popular Vibecoding & AI Engineering Festival is bringing together vibecoders and AI engineers under one roof. Attendees can expect a blend of project showcases and hands-on, step-by-step sessions designed to turn inspiration into real progress. LUMI AI Factory is a co-organiser of this event.

The entry to the event is free , but capacity is limited. Speaker announcements will be revealed soon. It is advised to bring a partner along—having a sparring partner makes it much easier to test ideas, solve problems, and continue building together after the event.

The festival takes place at the **LUMI AI Factory Hub Ostrava** ( alias IT4I AI Factory hub), where members of the LUMI AI Factory team from the IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center will also be present.

## More information

The festival has currently reached capacity, but interested attendees can still join the waiting list. Should a place become available, it will be offered to those on the list
