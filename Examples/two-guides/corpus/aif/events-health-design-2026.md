# Health Design 2026

![Health design 2026](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Image-22.10.2026-at-12.56-960x540.png)

## Event details

Date: 28–29.10.2026

Locations:

**28.10.** University of Helsinki main building: Great Hall & Agora
**29.10.** Aalto University main building Dipoli

Meet LUMI AI Factory at Health Design 2026!

Explore how AI changes healthcare and discover the latest in health technology and service innovation at this two-day event. Visit the LUMI AI Factory booth on both event days to discuss your ideas, challenges, and opportunities in AI, from early-stage exploration to scaling impactful solutions.

On 29 October, our Health & Life Sciences Business Domain Manager, Henrik Honkanen, will join the panel discussion “What AI Can and Cannot Solve in Healthcare.”

Come and meet us to find out how we can help you boost your AI innovation efforts!
