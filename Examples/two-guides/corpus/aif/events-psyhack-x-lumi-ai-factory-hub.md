# PsyHack x LUMI AI Factory Hub: Power of data

![PsyHack x LUMI AI Factory](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Untitled-design-27.jpg)

## Event details

Place: LUMI AI Factory Hub Otaniemi, Aalto University

Maarintie 8, Espoo, 4th floor, 02150 Espoo

Date: 19.3.2026

Time: 17.00-19.00 EET

What is data — really?

Data shapes decisions, innovation, and entire industries. But what actually is data? What can we truly do with it?
How do AI tools turn raw information into meaningful insight — and can it all be done ethically?

In this PsyHack pre-event, we explore the foundations and the future of data: from technical applications to ethical dilemmas, and from machine learning to psychological practice. A little preview of PsyHack.

Whether you’re curious about AI, data ethics, psychology, innovation — or simply looking for inspiring conversations and good refreshments — this evening is for you.
