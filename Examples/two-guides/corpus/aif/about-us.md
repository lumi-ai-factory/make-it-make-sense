# Welcome to LUMI AI Factory!

## Accelerating Europe’s AI Innovation

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LUMI-AIFlogo_1200x630-800x420.jpg)

The LUMI AI Factory is a leading-edge service infrastructure and expert support centre designed to accelerate AI innovation across Europe. We deliver a seamless, open AI solution that integrates world-class supercomputing, high-value data, and top-tier AI expertise.

We aim to empower you, whether you’re an AI startup, SME, researcher, or organisation, to develop innovative AI models and applications through a comprehensive and accessible service infrastructure. Whether you’re just starting with high-performance computing (HPC) or you are an experienced AI developer, our services are customised to meet your needs. With hands-on expert support, tailored training, and advanced computing resources, you gain the tools, knowledge, and guidance to grow your skills, expand your expertise, and achieve your goals with our experts by your side.

## Empowering Europe’s AI Ecosystem

By building on the LUMI supercomputer, adding an AI-optimised system, and exploring quantum computing for AI workloads, we provide a world-class environment for innovation. The LUMI AI Factory is also part of Europe’s [network of AI Factories](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/ai-factories_en), and stands out with its unique combination of cutting-edge infrastructure and expertise to help users achieve their AI ambitions. In industries such as manufacturing, engineering, life sciences, and pharmaceuticals, and more, the adoption of AI is expected to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and create new opportunities across domains and sectors. Regardless of your industry or background, whether you are a European startup, SME, academic institution, or industrial company, you are all welcome to reach out to us.

## Benefits to society

LUMI AI Factory is designed with sustainability at its core. As one of the world’s most energy-efficient and eco-friendly large-scale HPC and AI infrastructures, it ensures that cutting-edge technology is developed responsibly benefiting both people and the planet.

We seek to create positive impacts on society and the environment through all our efforts. We contribute to Europe’s competitiveness by enabling innovation in sectors critical to economic growth, sustainability, and societal value. By advancing AI adoption and integration, we facilitate breakthroughs that have the potential to improve our quality of life, create jobs, and address pressing global challenges.
