# Survey: AI training needs for industry

1 min read

31.10.2025

 ![We want to hear from you!](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/SoMe_1200X630_v2-960x540.jpg)

We are conducting a survey in collaboration with [DIMECC Oy](https://www.dimecc.com/) to develop AI trainings for the Finnish industry.

Finnish industry representatives – we want to hear form you about your AI related training needs, be it introduction to machine and deep learning, generative AI, hands-on tutorials, AI use cases in different industry areas, or something else entirely. The survey will also collect information on training format and language preferences.

The survey is anonymous and the information collected will be used to plan trainings based on industry needs. Answering will take a maximum of 2 minutes. [Fill in the survey here](https://link.webropolsurveys.com/Participation/Public/c164eba9-9983-4ca8-8a14-4a27ce863281?displayId=Fin3404362)!
