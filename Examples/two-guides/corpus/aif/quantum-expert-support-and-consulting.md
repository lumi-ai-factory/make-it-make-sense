# Quantum expert support and consulting

*Online expert support and consultation for implementing quantum and hybrid algorithms on LUMI-IQ computing environment.*

## Service features

Quantum computing and hybrid quantum, HPC and AI workflows are unfamiliar territory for most, and the skill gap between a promising idea and a running experiment can be wide. With Expert support, you work directly with quantum, HPC and AI specialists to create tailored solutions for your target computing environment.

The service covers the full path from first question to hands-on results, and includes:

- Consultation to assess whether your problem is a good fit for quantum or quantum-enhanced approaches, frame it to suit the hardware, and choose and access the right resources
- Guidance and practical help getting started with quantum and hybrid workflows
- Support with the LUMI-IQ software stack and software installation
- Support with project applications for LUMI-IQ
- Online documentation and training materials to help you get up and running in the LUMI-IQ environment

Expert support works hand in hand with the Computing resources service to help you make the most of your computing time.

## Target groups

Users needing advice in uptake of LUMI-IQ or in implementing, running and optimising their quantum or hybrid computing project on LUMI-IQ computing environment.
