# LUMI AI Factory Open Data Hackathon

## Event details

Location: Kumpula Campus, Helsinki, Finland

Date: 26.1.2026

Time: 10:00–16:00 EET

Join us for a full day hackathon on using AI methods for your own projects using Open Data. Learn new ways to use artificial intelligence and high-performance computing to create your own projects and pitch them to the organizers.

The hackathon is held at Kumpula Campus, Physicum D101. Teams of 3-5 students are formed at the start of the hackathon. The hackathon is also open to other participants.
