# AI Walk & Talk

![AI_Walk_n_Talk](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI_Walk_n_Talk.avif)

AI\_Walk\_n\_Talk\_event\_Image

## Event details

Place: Ostrava-Poruba, Czechia

Date: 18.6.2026

Time:
16:00-19:00

The best ideas do not have to come from formal conferences, they can also emerge naturally on a stroll through the forest. Participants will have the opportunity to chat about AI trends and everyday tech during a relaxed walk alongside experts from the **LUMI AI Factory from the IT4Innovations National Supercomputing Center**. The afternoon will then reach it final destination at the brand-new [Czechia LUMI AI Factory Hub](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/czechia-lumi-ai-factory-hub/) where a practical workshop will take place.

The meetup will feature **Matej Novak** and **Radek Halfar** who will focus on:

- **Training custom AI models** on IT4Innovations supercomputers.
- **Maximising efficiency and performance** when utilising HPC for model training.
- **Securely deploying AI** into real-world operations.
- **Exploring collaboration opportunities** with IT4Innovations to boost research, business, and technological innovation.

This event is ideal for anyone passionate about AI, new technologies, and connecting with like-minded innovators.
