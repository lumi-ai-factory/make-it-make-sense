# ICT KKDS Conference Prague 2026

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ICT_KKDS_Header-960x540.png)

## Event details

Location:
National Technical Library Prague,
Technická 2710/6, 160 80 Praha 6, Czechia

Date: 5.11.2026

The ICT Conference KKDS 2026, building on a 19-year tradition, will address the strategic alignment of technology, cyber security, and regulatory compliance.

Organised under the auspices of the Ministry of Industry and Trade, the event offers ICT managers and business leaders actionable insights into essential European legislation—including NIS2, GDPR, the AI Act, and the Cyber Resilience Act. Attendees will gain a comprehensive view on managing reliable, cost-effective infrastructure while successfully mitigating cyber risks and meeting complex legal obligations.

Beyond the expert panel discussions and networking opportunities, participants can engage directly with industry specialists to solve specific operational challenges. At the LUMI AI Factory stand, visitors are warmly invited to stop by to explore opportunities for prospective collaboration, discuss legal frameworks, and consult on strategic cyber security solutions tailored to their business needs.

## More information

The conference is still open for registration. The event will be held predominantly in Czech.
