# Innovation Week 2026

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/InnovationWeek_Header-960x540.png)

## Innovation Week

Location: CSOB Campus, Prague, Czechia

Date: 1.–3.10.2026

Europe’s largest event on innovations & AI. Together with global trailblazers, innovators, and thought leaders, The event explores ideas that shape the near future of our planet and redefine what’s possible.

The 11th edition will showcase the latest advancements in artificial intelligence, advanced robotics, space technologies, and cybersecurity. It explores how cutting-edge research transitions from laboratories into practical, real-world applications and commercial solutions.

The event is designed for business leaders, entrepreneurs, tech innovators, researchers, and startups seeking to discover emerging trends, establish strategic partnerships, and secure investment opportunities.While the initial days cater primarily to industry professionals and decision-makers, the programme also welcomes the wider public and tech enthusiasts eager to engage with emerging technologies and network within the regional innovation ecosystem.

LUMI AI Factory will be present at the event with its own dedicated booth, where visitors can consult on the possibilities of utilising the services and advanced infrastructure offered by this project.
