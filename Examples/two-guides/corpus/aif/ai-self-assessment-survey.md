# AI self-assessment survey

## Let’s get started

This is LUMI AI Factory’s AI self-assessment survey. The survey contains questions about your service needs and your organisation’s AI and HPC expertise. In addition, we will ask about your hopes in terms of growing your organisation’s  AI capabilities. The survey consists of three sections and filling in the survey will take 5-10 minutes.

After you have filled out the survey, a Customer Manager will contact you and help you with the next steps of your AI journey, e.g., to guide you towards specific experts and/or services.
