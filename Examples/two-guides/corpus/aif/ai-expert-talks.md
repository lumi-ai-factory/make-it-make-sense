# AI expert talks

*Bring trusted, practical AI expertise to your event. LUMI AI Factory provides experienced AI and data experts as keynote speakers or guest presenters, helping your audience understand how to apply AI in real-world contexts and explore opportunities with LUMI AI Factory solutions.*

## Service features

Enhance your event with expert-led AI content that is tailored, insightful, and directly relevant to your audience’s needs. LUMI AI Factory speakers deliver engaging presentations that combine technical expertise, practical examples, and real-world perspectives on AI adoption.

**What you can expect:**

- High-quality presentations from experienced AI and data experts
- Objective, vendor-neutral content based on real-world experience
- Flexible presentation formats (5–30 minutes)
- Content tailored to your audience (from beginner to specialised level)
- Practical examples and insights into real AI use cases across industries

**Example topics include:**

- Introduction to LUMI AI Factory
- Applying AI in business
- AI for research and scientific computing
- AI in health and life sciences
- Thematic topics such as trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, and data-driven innovation

**Access/Output Requirements**

- The default language for presentations is English.
- The service availability may be limited due to our experts’ schedules.

## Target groups

- Startups and SMEs
- Large industry
- Public organisations
- RPOs (Research performing organisations)

## Pricing

We do not charge for presentations, but reimbursement of travel expenses and other additional expenses need to be agreed case by case.

## Request process

Request this service via the contact form below (select “AI ecosystem and networking” as inquiry type).

Please include in your application the following information: event organiser’s name, event name, date and place, expected talk description (topic, format, special requirements etc.), audience level of knowledge, type of event: online or on-site.
