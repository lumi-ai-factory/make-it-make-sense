# Match XR 2025

![Match XR 2025 Event Image](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Untitled-design-15-960x540.jpg)

## Event details

Place:  Helsinki XR Center, Helsinki, Finland

Date:18.11.2025

Time: 11:00 – 18:00 EET

Join us at Match XR 2025!

Match XR is the biggest one-day XR & emerging tech event in the Nordics. Once a year the event assembles the industry’s stakeholders, opinion leaders and peers. We focus on the current state of Extended Reality (XR), Spatial Computing, Web3, AI and enabling technologies.

At Match XR, we’re excited to share how LUMI AI Factory’s free services can support startups – not to be missed! Come and visit us at booth 31 to learn how the LUMI AI Factory, one of Europe’s first AI factories, is accelerating AI innovation for companies. By providing open access to the world-class EuroHPC LUMI supercomputer, high-quality open data, and cutting-edge AI expertise, we enable startups, SMEs, and researchers to build advanced AI models and applications.

Stop by, get inspired, and explore how supercomputing and AI come together with LUMI AI Factory!
