# Connect and grow with new scientific and business partners

AI ecosystem and networking

While individual players can launch remarkable innovations in AI, nobody can lead the way in global AI transformation alone. The LUMI AI Factory ecosystem offers you many contact points to national, regional and international stakeholders ranging from AI research and education partners to local networks of startups, incubator programs and funders that could support your journey to AI.

[Physical AI Hubs in Finland and Czechia](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/hubs/) offer venues for face-to-face collaboration, events and co-creation.

European AI ecosystems are both local and global. Contact us to make the most of these opportunities for co-operation and growth!
