# AI Factories: Fostering Collaboration and Innovation

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/AdobeStock_387532307-960x540.jpeg)

## Event details

Location: Online

Date: 17.9.2026

Time: 9:00–10:30 CEST

Join the first DTA webinar in the series “AI Factories: Fostering Collaboration and Innovation” on 17 September 2026!

This 90-minute session will explore how AI Factories can support EDIHs, TEFs, SMEs, start-ups, public-sector organisations and other stakeholders in AI development, testing, adoption and deployment. The webinar will feature the LUMI AI Factory and the Luxembourg AI Factory, with practical examples, collaboration opportunities and a Q&A session.
