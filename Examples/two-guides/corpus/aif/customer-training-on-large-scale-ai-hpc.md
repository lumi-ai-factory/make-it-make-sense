# Customer training on large scale AI & HPC

*Build practical skills to work with AI and high-performance computing. With LUMI AI Factory training, you learn how to use advanced tools, develop models, and apply AI in real-world settings through hands-on experience.*

## Service features

- Training catalogue with regularly scheduled courses on topics such as deep learning and efficient use of HPC
- Hands-on exercises to build practical, real-world skills
- Flexible formats including online and on-site courses, workshops, webinars, hackathons, and self-paced modules
- Access to self-learning materials to support continuous development
- Domain-specific training options (e.g. health and life sciences)
- Quick and simple registration with fast confirmation
- Ability to participate in multiple training sessions based on your needs
- Possibility to connect training with additional consultation support if required
- Opportunity to discuss questions with trainers during or after sessions
- Option to suggest topics for future training based on your needs
