# Resource access proposal support

*Secure expert guidance to identify the right pathway and successfully apply for computing or data resources, saving you time and avoiding unsuccessful attempts.* *Receiving computing resources through EuroHPC or national calls would make it possible for you to develop and test your AI solution without HPC computing costs.*

## Service features

The support can include:

- **Expert consultation:** Online or email-based consultation with LUMI AI Factory experts tailored to your use case
- **Proposal revision:** Expert review and feedback on your application drafts to ensure they are ready for submission
- **Application templates:** Access to application templates to streamline your application process
- **Preparation guidance:** A comprehensive list of necessary information you need to prepare before applying
- **Broad infrastructure support:** Assistance is available even for proposals not directly connected to LUMI infrastructure

**Access/Output Requirements**

To receive support, you must provide to proposal:

- **Technical details:**Most importantly information on the software, frameworks, and tools currently used in your code base need to be known and prepared
- **Project intent:** A clear goal (e.g. Proof of Concept, scaling an existing project)
- **Work plan:** A general project plan and details of the team involved (Principal Investigator is required; other members can be added later)

## Target groups

Startups, SMEs, research institutions, universities, and public administration including those not specifically applying for the LUMI supercomputer.

## Pricing

The LUMI AI Factory consultation is open and free of charge for startups and SMEs from the EU Member States and countries associated with the Horizon Europe programme.

## Request process

1. **Initial request:** Fill out the contact form below (select “Expert support for AI business development” as inquiry type).

2. **Personal consultation:**Our Customer Manager will contact you to discuss your needs.

3. **Pathfinding & expert matching:** Your manager will help identify the most suitable resource pathway (e.g. EuroHPC JU, national calls) and connect you with a technical expert if a deep-dive review is required.
