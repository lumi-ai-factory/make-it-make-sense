## Terms of Use of LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of these Terms of Use, the following definitions apply:

**”Content”**
Means the Data Provider’s data that use or interact with the Services.

**“Dataset‑as‑a‑Service” or “DaaS”**
Means the service operated by LUMI AI Factory that enables Data Providers to publish datasets and related metadata for discovery and use by others.

**“Data Provider”**
Means any legal entity that publishes or makes available a dataset or a use copy of a dataset through the DaaS.

**“Data User”**
Means any legal entity or individual that accesses or uses datasets published through the DaaS.

**“LUMI-O”**
Object storage of LUMI that is used in Dataset-as-a-Service for storing data.

**“Metadata”**
Means descriptive information about a dataset, including but not limited to title, description, provenance, licensing, access conditions, and update information.

**“Use Copy”**
Means a copy of data made available solely for use, analysis, or research purposes, not for archiving or long‑term preservation.

### 3. Right to Use

The DaaS is intended solely for **sharing Use Copies of datasets** for discovery and reuse. The service does not function as a repository for long‑term preservation. The service is not meant for special categories of personal data.

All datasets published through the DaaS are made available for others to use. The Data Provider can define limitations, conditions, or restrictions (for example licensing, access conditions, or use limitations) for the datasets that they publish.

Uploading datasets to **LUMI‑O** and publishing Metadata via **Fairdata services** require authentication and compliance with the relevant login, registration, and user eligibility requirements, as defined in the applicable LUMI and Fairdata documentation listed in section 2 of this document.

LUMI AI Factory reserves the right to:

- Curate and decide which datasets are accepted for publication in the DaaS; and
- Reject or remove datasets that do not meet the published dataset selection criteria.

The curation process is done based on the general information of the dataset and its use cases, it does not asses the data itself. LUMI AI Factory disclaims any liability for the data included in the dataset. Further information on dataset selection criteria is published on the LUMI AI Factory webpagesand given to the Data Provider as part of the selection process.

The DaaS is provided **free of charge** for Data Providers whose datasets fulfill the applicable criteria.

- LUMI AI Factory reserves the right to delete the Metadata and any associated Use Copy stored in LUMI‑O;

LUMI AI Factory may suspend or terminate dataset publication immediately if required by law, policy, or in the event of misuse, security risks, or material breaches of these Terms of Use.

Termination of publication does not affect any separate agreements made directly between Data Providers and Data Users.
