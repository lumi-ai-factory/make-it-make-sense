# Learn the skills, tools and concepts of AI you need

Training and skills development

Build practical AI and HPC skills that match your business and research needs – from fundamentals of AI to quantum machine learning.

LUMI AI Factory delivers practical training to help you build the skills needed for advanced models and real-world innovation. The training activities help you understand how AI tools, data, and computing are used in real projects, through hands on learning and concrete examples. You gain relevant skills by combining guided training, self study, and applied exercises, so you can start using AI in your own work with a clear view of what is possible and what it requires. The program combines instructor-led and self-paced learning with hands-on practice, delivered through courses, workshops, hackathons, and curated materials.
