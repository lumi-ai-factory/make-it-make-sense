# Initial risk analysis consultation – EU AI Act Compliance Checker

*Get early clarity on your AI system’s regulatory obligations with a guided initial risk analysis aligned with the EU AI Act. In this expert-led consultation, you will identify your system’s likely risk level, understand applicable requirements, and receive actionable next steps to support compliant and trustworthy AI development.*

## Service features

This service helps you build a clear understanding of your AI system’s regulatory and ethical landscape early in the development process. Through a guided session using the EU AI Office’s Compliance Checker, you receive support in identifying your system’s likely risk classification and obligations under the EU AI Act. Early clarity reduces uncertainty, helps avoid costly redesigns and enables a smoother, more predictable path toward Trustworthy AI development.

The service includes a 1-hour online expert session.

You describe your AI system or model, its intended purpose and main use cases. During an online session, a LUMI AIF expert guides you through an initial risk analysis using the EU AI Office’s Compliance Checker. Together, we review key EU AI Act concepts and the roles involved in AI development. The outcome is a preliminary risk analysis that you can further refine and update as your project progresses.

**Disclaimer**: This service provides non‑binding expert guidance and does not constitute legal advice. Customers remain responsible for their own compliance decisions and regulatory interpretations.
