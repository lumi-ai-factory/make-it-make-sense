# LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service – how to get started

## Event details

Location: Online

Date: 10.6.2026

Time: 15:00-15:30 EEST

Join our 30-minute webinar on June 10 at 14:00 CEST / 15:00 EEST and discover how LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service can support your AI development — whether you’re a data user or a data provider.

If you’re interested in leveraging data for AI, this webinar will help you:

- explore the types of datasets available through the service
- learn how to get started quickly and easily
- understand the benefits of using LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service for your AI development needs

If you already have data and are looking for ways to make better use of it, you will also gain insights on:

- the opportunities the service offers for sharing and distributing data
- why sharing your datasets matters — and how to utilise LUMI AI Factory Dataset-as-a-Service to do it effectively to support AI innovation

We will also present a practical example of the Open Web Index dataset, already available through the service.

The session will conclude with a live Q&A. The presentation will be recorded and made available afterward.
