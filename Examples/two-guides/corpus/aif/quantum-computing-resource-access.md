# Quantum computing resource access

*Users get access to computing resources for quantum computer integrated to powerful HPC+AI+QC infrastructure, designed to support science and innovation.*

## Service features

Building and running quantum and hybrid workloads normally means investing in expensive, specialised infrastructure. Users of this service get access to a hybrid environment where quantum computing, high-performance computing and AI are tightly integrated, so you can develop, test and scale your work.

The service gives you access to:

- Quantum computing resources for the VLQ quantum computer, part of the EuroHPC ecosystem and connected to LUMI, and later for the LUMI-IQ platform
- High-performance computing and AI capacity on the LUMI supercomputer, and later the LUMI-AI supercomputer
- Hybrid workflows that combine quantum and classical HPC and AI in a single pipeline
- Quantum simulators for developing and testing your work before running it on physical quantum hardware
- Development environments and the software stack needed to build quantum and hybrid applications

Computing resources works hand in hand with the Expert support service — the specialists help you assess your problem, choose the right resources and get the most out of them.

## Target groups

Users seeking to run quantum and hybrid computing workloads on world class HPC+AI+QC infrastructure.

- Academic users can apply for national and EuroHOC allocations
- Industrial users can apply for EuroHPC allocations

## Pricing

The computing resources are provided open and free of charge to eligible users.
