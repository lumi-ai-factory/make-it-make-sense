# Scope and grow your AI idea

Expert support for AI business development

LUMI AI Factory’s expert support services for AI business development help you to assess and find clarity for the most suitable pathway in adopting AI, data and HPC in the LUMI AI Factory environment to reach your most advanced business goals.

Essential part of the services is the LUMI AI Factory’s expert support that helps you to evaluate the needed (AI and HPC) skills level, the type and scale of computing and data resources, and overall to figure out the most feasible approach to benefit and grow your business case.

The LUMI AI Factory consultation is open and free of charge for startups and SMEs from the EU Member States and countries associated with the Horizon Europe programme. Request consultation is available via web form.

## Our services for AI business development

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Start Your AI Journey](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/start-your-ai-journey/) | We provide a basic analysis for you to understand where your organisation stands now and which are the next steps to take with us. |
| [Resource access proposal support](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/resource-access-proposal-support/) | We will help you to find the most suitable computing or data resources that matches with your specific use case. |
| [Grant proposal consultation](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/grant-proposal-consultation/) | Get support to write technically feasible AI computing and data parts that are convincingly articulated in the grant applications. These may include national innovation funding or European programs such as Digital Europe Program or Horizon Europe. |
