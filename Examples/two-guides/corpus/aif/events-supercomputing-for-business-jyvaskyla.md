# Supercomputing for Business, Jyväskylä

![](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/csc.fi_Jkyla_896x448.jpg)

## Event details

Place: Jyväskylä, Finland

Date: 5.3.2026

Time: 8:30 – 13:00 EET

Join us at Supercomputing for Business, Jyväskylä and discover how our services can help you enhance your RDI work!

This seminar is intended for companies and researchers who are interested in enhancing their RDI activities with the help of high-performance computing (HPC). At the seminar, you will hear enlightening speeches from CSC experts. A company that has used the LUMI supercomputer will also tell you about the benefits they have achieved with LUMI.

The seminar will present:

- how LUMI can be used to enhance a company’s RDI work by utilizing HPC for computationally demanding AI development work, flow computing, demanding simulations and data analytics
- various financing options for companies
- practical instructions for using LUMI
- LUMI AI Factory services
- a company that has used LUMI in its RDI work
- the European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIH) operating in Finland

The seminar will be held in Finnish, but the presentation materials will be in English.
The event is free of charge and includes morning coffee and lunch.

The venue: AgAuditorio 3 in the Agora building at the University of Jyväskylä
