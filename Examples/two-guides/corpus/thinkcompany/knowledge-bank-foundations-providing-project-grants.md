## Foundations providing project grants

Looking for alternative ways to finance a business idea or project? Check out these grant opportunities.

## Finnish foundations

In Finland, foundations allocate tens of millions of euros annually to research and common-good activities. Get acquainted with the list of Finnish foundations, their operating principles, and criteria for awarding grants.

[Finnish foundations listing](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luokka:Suomalaiset_s%C3%A4%C3%A4ti%C3%B6t)

## [Association of Finnish Foundations](https://saatiotrahastot.fi/en/frontpage/)

The Association of Finnish Foundations monitors and defends the interests of its members, and serves as a trainer and expert, information provider and discussion forum, supporter, and publisher of research. The Association does not allocate grants itself, but it provides information for grant applicants. Learn more about the activities of the Association.

[Association of Finnish Foundations](https://saatiotrahastot.fi/en/for-grant-applicants/)
