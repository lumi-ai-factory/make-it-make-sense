# Contact us

You can shoot us a message in English or Finnish for example in regards to reserving our venues, attending events, recruitment or participation. We're happy to help with any questions or suggestions you may have – big or small!

We review new messages and will get back to you within a couple working days. Please describe in the subject box what your question is about, so we can direct the message to the right person.

Cheers!
