## Entrepreneurship Programmes

Find out more about entrepreneurial programmes that might help you take the next steps toward entrepreneurship.

## [HELSEED workshops](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

Helseed is Helsinki Think Company's programme, of which workshops aim to clarify ideas into fundable concepts. After the programme, those who submit their own plans will be given an investment from the University of Helsinki Fund. The programme consists of free workshops. Participation in the programme does not require participants to set up a business or submit a funding application. More information on HELSEED:

[HELSEED Workshops](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/helseed)

## [Ignite](https://ignite.aaltoes.com/) by Aaltoes

The annual Ignite business accelerator programme organized by Aaltoes, where students interested in entrepreneurship can test and develop their business idea in a safe environment for 10 weeks. Ignite offers participants support in e.g., team building, prototyping, building a business model, raising funding, and pitching an idea. Learn more about the programme and its workshops here:

[Ignite – Programme](https://ignite.aaltoes.com/program)

## [Urban Tech Helsinki](https://urbantechhelsinki.fi/)

Urban Tech Helsinki is an incubator focused on clean and sustainable urban solutions. The incubator helps you transform your business idea into a startup solving the challenges of modern cities. Read more about the incubator and its themes.

[Urban Tech Helsinki](https://urbantechhelsinki.fi/)
