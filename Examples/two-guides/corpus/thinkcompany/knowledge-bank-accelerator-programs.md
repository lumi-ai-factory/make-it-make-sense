## Accelerator programmes

Want to take your company to the next level with some help of a structured programme? Hold yourself accountable and meet the best advisors through early stage accelerator programmes!

## **WHAT IS AN ACCELERATOR**

“Startup accelerators support early-stage, growth-driven companies through education, mentorship, and financing. Startups enter accelerators for a fixed-period of time, and as part of a cohort of companies. The accelerator experience is a process of intense, rapid, and immersive education aimed at accelerating the life cycle of young innovative companies, compressing years’ worth of learning-by-doing into just a few months.” - HBR****

## [**Health Incubator Helsinki**](https://healthincubatorhelsinki.com/)

Health Incubator Helsinki is a three-year incubator programme for research-based health sector teams and start-ups. The program was initiated in 2019 by the City of Helsinki and the University of Helsinki to drive the transformation of health innovation, research findings and expertise into international business, and to attract new talent and companies to Helsinki. Read more about HIH

[Health Incubator Helsinki](https://healthincubatorhelsinki.com/)

## [**Helsinki Education Hub**](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/networks/helsinki-education-hub)

Helsinki Education Hub brings education innovators together under one EdTech roof. Helsinki Education Hub offers a collaboration place for creating innovative EdTech startups and strives to strengthen learning and education by improving EdTech startups' product development, innovations, and productivity. Read more about Helsinki Education Hub’s services and their 20-week EdTech Incubator Helsinki programme for startups and teams.

[Helsinki Education Hub](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/networks/helsinki-education-hub)
