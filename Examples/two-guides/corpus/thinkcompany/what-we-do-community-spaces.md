# COMMUNITY SPACES

## Locations on each campus

Our spaces are free of charge and open to all entrepreneurs and people hoping to turn their knowledge and skills into business ideas and concrete action. Whether you want to pop in every now and then or need a regular workspace, Helsinki Think Company is here for you.

**We no longer offer open coworking spaces with daily opening hours. However, you may visit the community spaces during our coworking events or other happenings, or apply for access to the spaces for independent use.**

Our community spaces are located at each University of Helsinki campus: City Center, Meilahti, Viikki and Kumpula

## How to access the spaces

### During Coworking Wednesdays, the concept is simple: come with your laptop, pick a seat and start working. It's free! Here's how to access different community spaces when they are vacant.

Independent use of the premises is intended for entrepreneurs, freelancers, companies, organisations etc., to work and organise events. You don't have to be a business owner or a student at the University of Helsinki to use our spaces – Helsinki Think Company is open to all aspiring entrepreneurs who want to develop their ideas into business.

The spaces have all the basic stuff you need in an office: wifi, printer, whiteboards, markers, post-its and so on. It’s all free of charge and you can work by yourself or bring a whole team with you. All of our spaces are accessible.

We offer event spaces in all four campuses, while Viikki and Kumpula available for independent access via Pindora code or keys.

Apply for access by sending us a message at helsinki@thinkcompany.fi.

Please follow these guidelines when using our coworking spaces:

1. Register at the door. This way we’ll be able to notify you if there’s a COVID case.

2. Remember to wash your hands often.

3. Keep a distance of more than 2 metres to others whenever possible. Wear a mask if you're not able to maintain safe distances. The spaces are open at half capacity, and entrance might be restricted if/when that capacity is reached.

4. Stay at home if you’re experiencing any flu symptoms or if you’ve travelled abroad within the past 10 days.

### Join Coworking Wednesdays!

### Want to explore the different community spaces? In need of a place to work on your projects? Look no further! You can do both at COWORKING WEDNESDAYS!

What are Coworking Wednesdays? A dedicated time and place, every week, at Think Company community spaces, when you can join us to work on your projects and ideas, while meeting new and old friends and colleagues. You can join us for the whole day (10–16), or pop by for a moment. The doors are open and coffee is served.

💛 See schedule and more info on [our instagram](https://instagram.com/helsinkithinkco)!

### A desk to call your own?

### Are you on the lookout for a cozy office space or a personal desk? We've got you covered.

Our Meilahti premises in Terkko Health Hub offer shared spaces with hot desks as well as furnished offices for companies that need a more closed space for teams. Companies in the health sector are prioritised. Being a member comes with perks, such as:

• 24/7 access
• your own secured locker
• use of the address for the company
• meeting rooms & phone booths

Visit the Terkko Health Hub website to learn more and apply for a membership!
