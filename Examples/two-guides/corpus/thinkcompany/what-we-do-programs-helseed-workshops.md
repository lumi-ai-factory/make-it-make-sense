## HELSEED workshops

**Taking the steps from a seed of an idea to a successful company can seem complex, but it doesn't have to be that way!** HELSEED is a programme designed for aspiring entrepreneurs who have the hunger to learn more, make progress on their business and apply for funding. We will be there for every step of the way! **Think of us as guides in the big forest of entrepreneurship.** We will direct you on your trail towards entrepreneurship all the way from developing your idea to getting to know your target audience. Then, we will guide you through testing your idea and getting it ready for you to be able to apply for funding. Ready to start the hike? *These workshops are organised in collaboration with the University of Helsinki, as a part of the wider* [*HELSEED programme*](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/strategy-economy-and-quality/university-finance/helseed)*. The workshops are open for everyone, but also designed to support students from the University of Helsinki on their journey in making a business plan and applying for HELSEED funding.*

## FAQ's

[**Who can participate?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

The answer is simple: everyone interested in our workshops can participate. We don’t have any requirements for a status of the participant or for baseline information.

[**Can I participate in the workshops on my own?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

Yes, you can participate in the workshops on your own! Same goes for the whole HELSEED programme, but if you are considering applying for funding, it is recommended that you find at least one other enthusiastic participant for your team. Founding a startup on your own is a big undertaking.

[**How do I participate in the workshops?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

You can pick the workshops you find interesting or attend to the whole series. Sign up to workshops you wish to attend by two days before the workshop will be held.

[**Who is organising the HELSEED Workshops?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

The HELSEED workshops are organised by Helsinki Think Company.

[**What is the language of the workshops?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

The language for all workshops is English.

[Can I get study credits from attending?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

Yes! Read more about study credits on the [University of Helsinki HELSEED page](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/innovations-and-cooperation/innovations-and-entrepreneurship/incubator-and-entrepreneurship-services/helseed-startup-programme).

[**Are the workshops online?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

All workshops of spring 2026 will be offline live events, located in Terkko Health Hub. More info on the registration website!

[**How are the workshops related to the HELSEED programme?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

The HELSEED programme is a startup entrepreneurship programme for students aiming to encourage them to develop their ideas towards startup companies. The premise of the programme is to draw up a business plan on the basis of an idea or for an existing startup, and the HELSEED workshops provide help for that. Possibility of early stage funding is offered for the most promising startups.

[**I want to participate in the HELSEED workshops, but not the whole programme, is that possible?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

Yes. Everyone is welcomed to HELSEED workshops and you can participate in the ones you find most interesting. If you want to apply for funding, we suggest taking part in all of the workshops!

[**I want to participate in the HELSEED programme, do I have to attend the workshops?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

Workshops are a voluntary part of the HELSEED programme. You can participate in all the other parts of the HELSEED programme, writing a business plan and applying for funding, without attending the HELSEED workshops.

[**How can I apply for funding?**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/603cd28863f223f1b6883a4c/60c32c12aa3e331866e19294_arrow-down-s-line%20(1).png)](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/programs/helseed-workshops)

HELSEED participants who are students at the University of Helsinki can apply for funding in spring 2026 from the University of Helsinki Funds. More precisely, one person in the applying team has to be an undergraduate or a postgraduate from the university. The funding process includes providing a business plan and pitching the business idea to the jury of the fund.

The funding decision is always taken on a case-by-case basis, meaning that several teams can be funded during the same autumn. The University of Helsinki Funds will make an initial investment of €10,000–€50,000 in the most interesting startups.
