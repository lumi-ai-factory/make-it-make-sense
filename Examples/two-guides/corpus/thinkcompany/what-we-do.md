# get involved

## Follow your curiosity

No matter what your level of knowledge or experience is in entrepreneurship, there’s always content and activities available to you. Find out what is most intriguing to you!

## Community spaces

We offer communal spaces at each of the University of Helsinki campuses. The spaces operate as community-driven event hubs as well as offices for companies and individuals in our community. Our spaces are open to all people wherever they are along their entrepreneurial path. We have all the basic stuff you need in an office – free of charge. With a membership, you can also unlock meeting rooms and personal desks at our Meilahti location.

[Learn more](https://www.thinkcompany.fi/what-we-do/community-spaces)
