## Office spaces

Are you looking for an office or a desk outside your home? Do you wish to share ideas and meet like-minded people during your workdays? We collected a list of coworking spaces below. Find out which space caters for your needs.

## [**Maria 01**](https://maria.io/) **- Startup campus, central Helsinki**

Maria 01 is the leading startup campus in the Nordic countries. Maria 01 provides a versatile work environment for technology startups, venture capitalists and large companies. Maria 01 regularly organizes various online, hybrid and local events for its network and for those interested in startup topics. Find out more about Maria01's workspaces and community.

[Maria 01](https://maria.io/campus/)

## [**Sofia Helsinki**](https://sofiahelsinki.fi/?gclid=Cj0KCQiAr8eqBhD3ARIsAIe-buNc0SY450Eb7N283kSZT4WfuZoNnjirA-IlCeaSJDU4T9C-8gHHlecaAoRfEALw_wcB)**, central Helsinki**

Sofia Helsinki’s offering consists of coworking spaces in the historical centre of Helsinki, events and meeting rooms and catering services for events and celebrations. Read more about Sofia Helsinki's diverse selection of services and workspace membership options.

[Sofia Helsinki](https://sofiahelsinki.fi/en/frontpage/)

## [**Terkko Health Hub**](https://terkko.fi/)**, Meilahti**

Terkko Health Hub is a startup community, a co-working space and an event venue focusing on health and life sciences. Terkko Health Hub is located in the heart of Helsinki’s medical campus in Meilahti, and provides various services and events for the community in collaboration with local and global partners. Read more about Terkko Health Hub.

[Terkko Health Hub](https://terkko.fi/)

## [**Wonderland work**](https://wonderlandwork.fi/)**, Vallila & Leppävaara**

Wonderland Work is a mix between a coworking space, restaurant, meeting rooms and event space located in Vallila, Helsinki and Leppävaara, Espoo. Find out more about Wonderland's offices, services and events.

[Wonderland Work](https://wonderlandwork.fi/en/frontpage/)

## [Workland](https://wrkland.com/c/espoo), Keilaniemi

Workland offers private offices, open area workstations and meeting rooms, covering all your office needs. Take a closer look at Workland’s services and memberships.

[Workland](https://wrkland.com/c/espoo)

## [**Spaces**](https://www.spacesworks.com/helsinki/?cust_source=google&cust_medium=cpc&cust_source_platform=GOOGLE&cust_campaign=FI+%3E+FI+%3E+OF+%3E+SM+%3E+Spaces+%3E+Helsinki&cust_adgroup=Coworking+%3E+Exact&cust_term=coworking+helsinki&gclid=Cj0KCQiAr8eqBhD3ARIsAIe-buNK5wDo6_126ZvHJ_y078nHL6FKrZrl2n-3eTqv6IdUmIxU_JRlUu0aAtGbEALw_wcB&gclsrc=aw.ds)**, Kluuvi, Kamppi, Ruoholahti, Lauttasaari**

Spaces operates around the world with multiple locations in Helsinki. Funnily enough, Spaces offers more than just office spaces, desks, virtual offices, and meeting rooms, but they also organize a wide range of events, business lunches and other community get-togethers. Look into Spaces in more detail.

[Spaces](https://www.spacesworks.com/helsinki/?cust_source=google&cust_medium=cpc&cust_source_platform=GOOGLE&cust_campaign=FI+%3E+FI+%3E+OF+%3E+SM+%3E+Spaces+%3E+Helsinki&cust_adgroup=Coworking+%3E+Exact&cust_term=coworking+helsinki&gclid=Cj0KCQiAr8eqBhD3ARIsAIe-buNK5wDo6_126ZvHJ_y078nHL6FKrZrl2n-3eTqv6IdUmIxU_JRlUu0aAtGbEALw_wcB&gclsrc=aw.ds)

## [**MOW**](https://mow.fi/) **Helsinki & Tampere**

MOW – mothership of work offers a wide range of workspaces at the heart of Helsinki and in Tampere. At MOW, one can choose from private rooms, fixed or hot desk areas and custom packages to suit the various needs of digital nomads. Read more about MOW’s offering, coworking opportunities and mid-workday napping possibilities.

[MOW](https://mow.fi/en/hub/mothership-helsinki)

## [**Epicenter**](https://www.weareepicenter.com/fibuPvfWo3gz1T7rdHr_C3wnY843uBOtk79J2W49_qhMG29MaOAhZC4ZIaAtpOEALw_wcB) **Helsinki**

Epicenter is a large community of digital scale-ups, corporates and entrepreneurs that offers an environment for innovation and growth in the heart of central Helsinki. Look into Epicenter’s various membership options, events and community activities or try their services for a month for free.

[Epicenter Helsinki](https://www.weareepicenter.com/fibuPvfWo3gz1T7rdHr_C3wnY843uBOtk79J2W49_qhMG29MaOAhZC4ZIaAtpOEALw_wcB)

## [VillageWorks](https://villageworks.com/en/), Central Helsinki

VillageWorks offers flexible, hassle-free workspace solutions in and around central Helsinki. Choose from fully serviced private offices, coworking spaces, and virtual office services, all designed to suit different ways of working. Need space for a meeting or event? They've got everything from smaller meeting rooms to large auditoriums. Discover VillageWorks and find the workspace that works for you!

[VillageWorks](https://villageworks.com/en/)
