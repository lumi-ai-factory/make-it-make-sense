## Support, consulting & advice

Sometimes getting advisors outside your own circles can benefit both you and your business. Here you can find some services that we recommend.

## **Helsinki Think Company 1 on 1 sparring**

****Do you have an idea but don’t quite know how to turn it into a business? Looking for co founders? Stuck figuring out how to contact or pitch your first customers? You can contact us anytime for individual sparring sessions! Send us a message at [support@thinkcompany.fi](mailto:support@thinkcompany.fi).

## [Business Helsinki](https://www.hel.fi/en/business-and-work/grow-your-business)- business advice and materials for starting a company

Business Helsinki promotes local entrepreneurship and startup activities through various partners and communities. Business Helsinki aims to make Helsinki known and to attract company founders and early-stage financiers from around the world. They offer free entrepreneurial support services and startup and ecosystem services for a variety of purposes. Check out Business Helsinki's comprehensive range of services and material bank.

Business Helsinki is a part of [Uusyrityskeskus](https://uusyrityskeskus.fi/en/homepage/) (Enterprise Agency). [Check out their guide for aspiring entrepreneurs.](https://uusyrityskeskus.fi/en/help-for-entrepreneurs/guide-to-entrepreneurship/)

[Business Helsinki](https://www.hel.fi/en/business-and-work/grow-your-business)

Economic Development Centre (formerly ELY-centre) offers many kinds of advice, educational and business development services as well as business funding. See also [Talous- ja velkaneuvonta yrityksille — financial advice for companies](https://elinvoimakeskus.fi/talous-ja-velkaneuvonta-yrityksille-ja-yrittajille).

## [Spark Finland](https://sparkfinland.fi/) - supporting health tech & life science project owners

SPARK Finland provides intensive support for increasing the maturity of ideas towards working and valuable solutions in the life science and health tech space. Their focus lies on supporting project owners with various resources needed to move forward with their ideas. Spark also provides open educational and networking events for a wider audience. Read more about Spark’s offering, projects and events.

[Spark Finland](https://sparkfinland.fi/about-us/)
